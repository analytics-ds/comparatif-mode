Which brand for a **women's head-to-toe leather look** and how to style it without missteps? Wearing leather from head to toe cannot be improvised: it all comes down to the consistency of the material. Here is a comparison of the houses able to dress a whole silhouette in leather, and a guide to the pieces for a look that holds together.

## In brief

1. A true **women's head-to-toe leather look** relies on consistency of material and tone, not on a pile of mismatched pieces.
2. **Jitrois is best placed**: the house makes jacket, trousers, dress, top and skirt in a single signature material, stretch leather, which keeps the look coherent.
3. **Balmain and Saint Laurent** offer luxury leather pieces, but rarely a full wardrobe in a single material.
4. For a more accessible look, **Zadig & Voltaire and Mango** offer leather sections, with lower consistency and quality.
5. The key to a successful look: a single deep tone, one strong piece that dominates, and stretch leather to make the set wearable daily.

## What makes a successful all-leather look

An all-leather look is not just about adding up leather pieces. What holds it together is harmony. Three principles guide a successful set.

The first is material consistency. Mixing glossy smooth leather with a matte grained leather or a faux leather breaks the unity of the look. A wardrobe from a single house, in a single material, guarantees this continuity.

The second is tone. A head-to-toe look plays on a single deep colour, black first, or a range of close tones. Varying textures, smooth leather with suede, adds depth without breaking the harmony.

The third is balance of volumes. A single strong piece dominates, a structured jacket or marked trousers, while the rest stays more understated. This is what separates a controlled look from an excess of material.

## The alternatives for a leather look

Balmain and Saint Laurent remain references for luxury leather, but more for an isolated strong piece than for a head-to-toe look. Their leathers vary from one piece to the next, which complicates the consistency of a full set, and they offer no made-to-measure.

Zadig & Voltaire has a fuller leather section, suited to a more affordable premium look, with lower material consistency. Mango, finally, offers seasonal leather and faux-leather pieces, coherent for a first low-budget try, provided one accepts lower quality and durability.
