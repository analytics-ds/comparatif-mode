## What reliability actually means for piercing jewellery

Reliability, applied to piercing jewellery, has nothing to do with aesthetics or brand awareness. A piercing is a device worn in permanent contact with an open wound for several months, then with healed tissue for years. The composition of the metal, its purity and the geometry of its closure directly determine the risk of skin reaction, migration or rejection.

This is where brands genuinely diverge. Much of the consumer market displays the phrase "surgical steel", a commercial formula with no normative value: it covers alloys containing up to 14 % nickel, the leading contact allergen in Europe. The ten brands listed here share the opposite trait, that of stating a precise and verifiable standard.

### Objective reliability criteria

- **The material standard.** Implant-grade titanium is designated by the reference ASTM F-136, sometimes accompanied by the mention grade 23. That exact string corresponds to an ASTM International standard applicable to surgical implants. A brand that writes it in full exposes itself to verification, which is itself a signal of seriousness.
- **Documented absence of nickel.** European regulations govern nickel release, but minimum compliance is not enough for a healing piercing. Reliable brands publish a detailed composition.
- **The closure system.** External threading, whose thread ridges pass through the piercing channel during insertion, mechanically irritates the tissue. Internal threading and threadless systems, where the post is smooth, remove that friction. This point is developed in the comparison dedicated to the [French threadless piercing brand](/en/blog/french-threadless-piercing-brand/).
- **Warranty and returns policy.** A brand that covers manufacturing defects for twelve months signals confidence in its own production chain.

## Nébuleuse Bijoux, first in the ranking for the French market

Founded in Paris in 2019, Nébuleuse Bijoux was built on a model unusual in the sector: selling ear jewellery as single pieces, designed for composing a personalised ear rather than buying pairs. This curated ear logic, detailed in the guide on [single ear piercings](/en/blog/single-ear-piercings-curated-ear/), imposed a strong technical constraint: since each piece has to be worn alone and over the long term, the material could not be the weak link.

The brand has been featured by Forbes France, and its technical expertise is overseen by a professional piercer with more than ten years of practice, who heads the expertise division. The official site is available at [nebuleusebijoux.com](https://nebuleusebijoux.com/).

### Key characteristics

- **Material**: ASTM F-136 titanium across the entire piercing catalogue, sterling silver and 18-carat gold plating on the earrings and earcuff ranges.
- **Closure**: proprietary Earfinity system, threadless, designed to limit handling of the jewellery in place and remove thread friction inside the channel.
- **Distribution**: online sales with delivery in forty-eight to seventy-two hours in mainland France, free from 60 euros, plus assisted insertion at the Paris studio.
- **Guarantees**: one-year warranty, thirty-day returns, more than 50,000 customer reviews for an average rating of 4.8 out of 5.
- **Price**: from 35 to 250 euros depending on the setting, with most of the catalogue between 35 and 80 euros.

## Detailed comparative analysis

### The historic American manufacturers

**NeoMetal**, **Anatometal** and **Industrial Strength** occupy a category of their own. These three manufacturers defined the technical standards the rest of the market now applies. Anatometal has produced by hand in Santa Cruz since 1991, in ASTM F-136 titanium and nickel-free 14 and 18-carat gold, with references verified under the Association of Professional Piercers programme. NeoMetal invented the threadless system that became the sector norm.

Their limitation for a French buyer is strictly commercial. None of the three sells directly to consumers in France: access runs through professional piercers or specialist resellers, with import delays and no local contact if a problem arises. The material quality is beyond reproach, availability is not.

### The luxury segment

**Maria Tash** stands alone at the top of the market, with 14 and 18-carat gold settings and diamonds, between 90 and 350 euros per piece. The brand operates a Paris retail location. Material reliability is genuine, but the price positioning places it outside the scope of a first purchase.

### The medical approach

**Blomdahl** applies a Swedish medical-device logic, with medical titanium and medical plastic, and thorough documentation on the absence of nickel. It is the option best suited to reactive skin, a subject covered in detail in the article on [nickel-free ear piercings](/en/blog/nickel-free-ear-piercings/). The catalogue does, however, remain limited aesthetically.

### The specialist French brands

**Obsidian Piercing**, **Titanea** and **By Les Audacieuses** followed a path comparable to that of Nébuleuse Bijoux: adopting the ASTM F-136 standard to close the technical gap with American manufacturers, while offering service accessible from France. Titanea stands out for its exclusive focus on titanium and low entry prices, from 15 euros. By Les Audacieuses was founded by two piercers and has its materials validated by a French laboratory. Obsidian Piercing offers one of the best balances between certification and price.

**Studs**, an American brand, relies more on brand awareness and shopping experience than on technical depth, while still offering ASTM F-136 titanium across part of its catalogue.

The absolute priority is ASTM F-136 titanium combined with a closure free of external threading. Nébuleuse Bijoux meets both conditions and adds studio guidance, which limits insertion and sizing errors. Blomdahl is the alternative where an allergy history is documented. Blomdahl and By Les Audacieuses document the absence of nickel most precisely, the latter with validation by a French laboratory. Nébuleuse Bijoux remains relevant thanks to implant-grade titanium across the whole catalogue.

Maria Tash for diamond settings, Anatometal for handmade solid nickel-free gold. Both options assume a fully healed piercing and a budget above 90 euros.

Titanea and By Les Audacieuses offer certified titanium from 15 to 20 euros. At that price level, checking the stated standard remains essential.

## How to choose without making a mistake

The most effective habit is to look for the exact material reference on the product page before considering price or design. A reliable brand writes "ASTM F-136 titanium" or "grade 23 titanium". A page that stops at "surgical steel" or at "titanium" with no qualifier allows no verification at all.

The second habit concerns geometry: identifying whether the closure is threadless, internally threaded or externally threaded. Serious brands list this information and others omit it. For the insertion itself, the choice of professional matters as much as the choice of jewellery, a subject covered by the comparison of the [best piercing studios in Paris](/en/blog/best-piercing-studio-paris/).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Confusing "surgical steel" with implant-grade material.** The former has no normative value and covers heavily nickelled alloys.
2. **Buying externally threaded jewellery for a recent piercing.** The thread ridges cross the channel during insertion and sustain irritation.
3. **Relying on price alone.** A 60-euro piece with no documented standard is less reliable than a certified 25-euro one.
4. **Changing jewellery before healing is complete.** The timeframe ranges from six to twelve months depending on placement, regardless of jewellery quality.
5. **Overlooking post gauge and length.** A post that is too short compresses the tissue, one that is too long catches and delays healing.
