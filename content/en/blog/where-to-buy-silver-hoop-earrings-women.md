## Practical tips for choosing women's silver hoop earrings online

Beyond the choice of site, several technical criteria help avoid disappointment on receipt and secure the purchase.

### Check the title and hallmark

Silver sold in France as sterling silver 925 must bear the Minerva head hallmark, mandatory for jewelry titled at 925 thousandths and more. The merchant site must indicate:

- Exact title (925/1000 for sterling silver)
- Unit weight in grams (indicator of strength and amount of metal)
- Inner diameter in millimeters (actual size to the ear)
- Wire or tube thickness in millimeters (between 1 and 4 mm depending on format)
- Type of clasp (snap, hinge, retractable post, screw)

### Mistakes to avoid when buying silver hoop earrings for women

1. Buying silver plated without checking the title. "Silver plated" is not regulated like sterling silver 925, its lifespan is much shorter.
2. Neglecting the warranty. A warranty of less than 2 years against tarnishing is a warning sign on the French market.
3. Ignoring the alloy composition in case of sensitive skin. Alloys containing nickel can cause allergies. Check the mention "nickel-free" or "hypoallergenic" for skin at risk.
4. Choosing a diameter without measuring on yourself. A 25 mm hoop renders very differently from a 40 mm one on the ear. Measuring or basing on a pair already worn is the best method.
