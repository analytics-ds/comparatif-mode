## Why choosing a men's sweater depends as much on budget as on fabric

A men's sweater is a piece worn several times a week for three quarters of the year, which makes its **value for money** particularly sensitive: a sweater that is too thin loses its shape after a few washes, while an overly expensive sweater for a basic everyday use does not always make sense. The question of a **quality, affordable men's sweater** is therefore less about finding the lowest price than about finding the right balance between fabric, care and budget.

### What makes a men's sweater good quality

- **Composition**: cotton, merino wool or a cashmere blend, each fabric has a different use and care routine.
- **Knit density**: a sweater that is too thin becomes see-through and loses its shape faster.
- **Finish**: seams, cuffs and collar ribbing, which need to hold up over time.
- **Price against use**: a sweater worn daily justifies a different investment than one worn occasionally.

## Practical tips for choosing a men's sweater

Before buying, it is worth checking the exact composition on the product label rather than relying on the photo alone, and planning care suited to the chosen fabric. A cotton sweater washes easily in a machine, while a merino wool or cashmere blend sweater needs a cold wash and flat drying, as detailed in our guide on [how to wash a cashmere sweater without damaging it](/en/blog/how-to-wash-cashmere-sweater/).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing based on price alone without checking the exact composition.
2. Washing a wool or cashmere sweater on a hot machine cycle, which felts and shrinks it.
3. Overlooking the sweater's fit relative to what it will be worn with, especially over a shirt.
