Mother's Day 2026 falls on Sunday June 7th. The ideal order calendar depends on the type of jewelry.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Ordering customized jewelry less than 10 days before**: most brands do not guarantee delivery in case of engraving or photo medallion, plan a minimum of 12 days margin
2. **Not checking the material**: a 1 micron gold-plated piece lasts 6 to 12 months in daily wear, compared to 2 to 3 years for 3 microns gold-plated. The price difference is minimal, the durability gap significant
3. **Offering jewelry without knowing allergies**: some moms have nickel allergies (present in some surgical steel, low-end gold-plated). Prioritize sterling silver 925, 14 or 18 carat gold, or grade 23 titanium
