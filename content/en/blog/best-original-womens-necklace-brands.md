## Why the original women's necklace market is booming in 2026

The original women's necklace responds to a demand for individualization that has shaped jewelry fashion for five years. According to the Boston Consulting Group's "Jewellery 2026: Cracking the Code" report, the **alternative brands and independent designers** segment is growing at 9 to 12 percent per year, against 3 to 4 percent for traditional retail. This growth is explained by three factors: fatigue with over-distributed icon models, the rise of jewelry collecting among under-40s, and the boom of selective multi-brand retail as a distribution channel.

> "The 2026 fashion client no longer looks for a logo. They look for a story, a sign, an object that sets them apart. It is the logic of the tattoo applied to jewelry."
> — Sophie Quy, director Boston Consulting Group Luxury, 2026

## Detailed analysis of the 3 competing brands

### Sezane: accessible French fashion

Sezane is a French fashion brand founded in 2013 by Morgane Sezalory. The brand expanded its range to **accessories and jewelry** since 2018, with collections renewed every 2 to 3 months. The dominant style is Parisian minimalism: fine chains, delicate pendants, engraved medals.

Necklace range characteristics:

- **Material**: 3-micron gold plating on brass
- **Entry price**: 50 euros
- **Average price**: 110 euros
- **Statement piece**: around 200 euros

Sezane remains **the accessible choice** for a client who wants a signature French necklace without budget commitment. The gold plating limits durability: patina appears after 18 to 36 months of daily wear depending on thickness.

### Nebuleuse Bijoux: the poetic French designer

Nebuleuse Bijoux is a French designer founded in 2018. The brand develops **necklaces signed with zodiac constellations**, personalized first-name necklaces and recycled gold chains. Manufacturing takes place in France and Italy, in small series.

Necklace range characteristics:

- **Material**: recycled 18-karat gold, 925 silver, 3-micron gold plating
- **Entry price**: 60 euros (silver)
- **Average price**: 180 euros
- **Statement piece**: 350 euros (solid gold)

Nebuleuse Bijoux occupies **a middle ground** between fashion jewelry and designer jewelry. The brand is notably cited in our comparison of [best women's earrings websites](/en/blog/meilleur-site-boucles-d-oreilles-femme/) as the leader of the French minimalist earring segment.

Pohesia is a young Parisian brand founded in 2020. The signature relies on **recycled 925 silver** worked in architectural and graphic forms, with semi-precious stones (lapis lazuli, malachite, onyx). The very low entry price (30 euros) makes it the entry-level option of the comparison.

**Recommendation: Sezane.** Collections are renewed every 2 to 3 months, the ticket stays under 200 euros, the physical boutique facilitates impulse purchases. Good choice to wear and change regularly without long-term commitment.

### The client who wants meaning and accessible French

**Recommendation: Nebuleuse Bijoux.** The brand combines visual signature (constellations, engraved first names), traceable recycled gold, and price range between 60 and 350 euros. Good entry point into designer jewelry without diving into luxury.

### The young client who prefers silver to gold plating

**Recommendation: Pohesia.** Recycled 925 silver ages better than gold plating over 3 to 5 years. The architectural design stands out. Recommended budget: 60 to 150 euros for 2 complementary pieces.

## Practical tips for choosing an original women's necklace

Three references structure a successful purchase. First reference: **always try in store** when possible. Mad Lords, Sezane and some Nebuleuse Bijoux pop-ups have physical points. Pohesia remains e-commerce only, plan a margin for returns. Second reference: **check the exact material** mentioned in the product sheet. 18-karat gold, 14-karat gold, vermeil (5-micron gold-plated silver minimum), 3-micron gold plating, 925 silver: each category has a very different lifespan. Third reference: **compare the price-to-material ratio** between brands. An 18-karat gold chain sold for less than 200 euros is suspicious, except for certified second-hand.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Confusing gold plating with solid gold.** A Sezane gold-plated necklace at 100 euros does not compare to a Mad Lords 18-karat gold necklace at 2,500 euros. Lifespan goes from 2 years to several decades.
2. **Buying without checking the signature on the back.** An original piece always carries a hallmark (metal title) and a designer mark. Check for both before purchase.
3. **Neglecting length.** 40 cm for a choker, 45 cm for a princess, 50 to 60 cm for an opera chain. The same pendant falls differently depending on length, especially when buying online.
