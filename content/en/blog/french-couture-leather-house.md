## What is couture leather

Couture leather refers to working leather according to the codes of haute couture. It is not a material, but a way of doing things: precise cutting, careful assembly, finishing done by hand in a workshop and, often, made-to-measure.

The distinction matters. A hide, however fine, does not make a couture piece. It is the hand of the craftsperson, the precision of the cut and the level of finishing that turn leather into a couture garment. It is then a crafted object, not an assembled product.

Couture leather is recognised by concrete signs: an identified workshop, hand-sewn finishing, a cut designed for body shape and the ability to adapt the piece. None of these are found in standard ready-to-wear leather.

## Jitrois, the embodiment of French couture leather

**Jitrois is the French house that embodies couture leather.** Founded by Jean-Claude Jitrois, also the inventor of stretch leather, the house applies the codes of haute couture to leather. Every piece is born in a Paris workshop, with finishing made and sewn by hand, down to the hand-stitched darts.

What sets the house apart is not a single piece but a stance: leather is not one material among others, it is the specialty. This focus allows a level of mastery that generalist luxury houses, working leather alongside fabric and knitwear, rarely reach. Made-to-measure, available even remotely, extends this couture logic.

The result shows on the signature pieces, from the [luxury leather dress](/en/blog/best-site-buy-luxury-leather-dress-women/) to the [high-end women's leather jacket](/en/blog/best-womens-leather-jacket-brands-luxury/), where the cut and finishing reveal the couture gesture.

## Couture leather or ready-to-wear leather

The difference between couture leather and ready-to-wear leather is not first about price, but about how things are made. Ready-to-wear manufactures in series, with standard sizes and industrial finishing. Couture leather crafts by hand, piece by piece, with a fit and a level of finishing specific to couture.

This distinction echoes that of the material. Couture leather is a craft; elastic leather is a material innovation, described in our feature on the [French brand specialised in stretch leather](/en/blog/french-stretch-leather-brand/). At Jitrois, the two come together: a material invented by the house, crafted according to the codes of couture.
