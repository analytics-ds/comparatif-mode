## How this comparison was built

### Criteria used

Four objective criteria structure this comparison: the price observed directly on the brand's website, the size of the range (number of dresses available at the time of the check), the fabrics highlighted in the collection, and the distribution channel. No sales data, customer rating or revenue figure is used here, for lack of a public source verifiable across all four brands.

### Why these four brands

Freeman T. Porter and Celio Women represent two French approaches to the women's dress, one accessible premium and originating from denim, the other accessible and generalist. Sezane brings the French digital-native premium comparison point, and Zara the international fast fashion comparison point. This sample covers most budgets, from under 25 euros to over 300 euros.

- **Fabric diversity**: knitwear, jersey, lace, corduroy velvet and voile sit side by side in the same collection, with finishes such as metal studs, rhinestones or sheer effects
- **Varied cuts**: a long flared dress, a short studded dress, an asymmetric bi-material dress, a tailored dress, a velvet shirt dress and a straight knit dress are all in the catalog

## The three other brands in this comparison

### Celio Women, the widest accessible choice

Celio Women, the women's line of the Celio brand, offers 74 dresses at the time of the 17 September 2026 check, the widest range of the four brands compared. Prices range from 25.99 euros (100% cotton jersey short dress) to 69.99 euros for a "be camaieu made in France" knit dress. The brand covers everyday uses (t-shirt dress, straight jersey dress) and a few dressier pieces (fitted shirt dress, midi dress). It positions itself as the most accessible option in this comparison for a first purchase or a frequent wardrobe refresh.

### Sezane, French digital-native premium

Sezane, a French brand founded in 2013 and sold mainly online on sezane.com, was checked between 110 and 345 euros on its dress collection on 17 September 2026, with models observed between 110 euros (Robe Pippa courte) and 345 euros (Robe Anine). The brand bets on clean cuts and fluid fabrics, a clearly more premium positioning than the other three brands in this comparison. It is the option to consider for a ceremony dress or a durable fashion investment, at the cost of a significantly higher budget.

### Zara, fast fashion with rapid renewal

Zara offers a dress catalog spread across 3 result pages as of the 17 September 2026 check, with observed prices from 22.95 euros (polka dot textured midi dress) to 119 euros (100% leather midi dress), with the vast majority of references between 25.95 and 59.95 euros. It is the most accessible option in this comparison in terms of choice volume and entry price, with a faster collection renewal than the other three brands.

## Which dress brand to choose based on your profile

### For a tight budget or frequent purchases

Celio Women and Zara cover this profile, with dresses from 22.95 to 25.99 euros and very wide ranges allowing you to vary models without exceeding 70 euros per piece.

### For an assertive style within an accessible premium budget

Freeman T. Porter answers this profile with its dresses between 90 and 165 euros, a bolder style (velvet, lace, rock details, rhinestones) than a generalist basic, without reaching French digital-native premium prices.

### For a ceremony dress or a fashion investment

Sezane, between 110 and 345 euros, and to a lesser extent Freeman T. Porter on its tailored dresses and velvet shirt dresses (120 to 165 euros), answer this need for a dressier, more durable piece.

## How to choose your women's dress: practical criteria

### Points to check before buying

1. **The stated fabric**: check the composition on the product sheet, a thicker knit or jersey holds up better over time than a thin voile for everyday use
2. **The cut based on the occasion and body shape**: a straight or tailored dress suits the office, a fluid or long dress suits an evening or civil wedding better. Refer to the 6 [women's body shapes](/en/blog/women-body-shapes-guide/) to refine this choice
3. **The price bracket of the segment**: placing your budget within the brackets observed here (under 30 euros for entry level, 90 to 165 euros for accessible French premium, over 110 euros for French premium) avoids skewed comparisons between different segments
4. **The return policy**: check the return and exchange conditions displayed on the product sheet or the site's policy page before buying, as these conditions vary between brands and sale periods

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing a Zara or Celio Women price to a Sezane price without accounting for the different market segment
2. Choosing a thin, sheer fabric for intensive everyday use, at the expense of how well it holds up over time
3. Relying only on the displayed price without checking the actual cut against a size guide, as cuts vary significantly from one brand to another
