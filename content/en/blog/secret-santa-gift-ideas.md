Secret Santa gift idea lists nearly all look the same. They pile up gadgets under 15 euros without saying which ones will last more than a week on a desk.

This guide takes a different approach. It starts from the categories that really work in a Secret Santa, gives the observed prices, then compares retailers according to the kind of gift you are after. Prices quoted for Lulli sur la Toile were checked product by product on the website on 28 September 2026, and every piece mentioned was in stock on that date. The volumes of the other retailers were recorded the same day on their Secret Santa pages, when they were accessible.

Three reasons, which come down to what a Secret Santa really requires.

The first is being able to stay within budget without giving a gadget. The second is the network of boutiques. ### Where the others do better

A ranking that nuances nothing helps nobody, so it is worth saying.

If the group has set a 10 euro cap, Lulli sur la Toile has almost nothing to offer apart from a 10 euro tote bag. Du Bruit dans la Cuisine, with most of its Secret Santa collection under 20 euros, or Cultura, with its ideas under 15 euros, are then much better suited. ## Ideas by category

### Skincare and beauty, the risk-free gift

This is the safest category for a Secret Santa. Nobody turns down good skincare, and the budget is easy to hold.

At [La Crique Beauty](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/accessoires/bien-etre.html), the Booster d'Éclat Soleil serum costs 22 euros. Enriched with hyaluronic acid, it gives a natural sun-kissed glow, and it contains 99% ingredients of natural origin. At La Bonne Brosse, the wide acetate detangling comb costs 38 euros, in Cherry Red, Cream White or Emerald Green, and the Cair pre-shampoo repairing mask, made with rice water and free from silicone and sulphates, 48 euros.

### Home fragrance, the other safe bet

It depends neither on a size nor on a fashion style, which makes it the ideal gift for someone you barely know. At Wijck, the London Architect room fragrance costs 15 euros in 60 ml, and the 200 ml diffusers, in London Architect or Paris Urbanist, 35 euros. For a 50 euro cap, Juliette Has a Gun's Not a Room Spray, in 200 ml, is right on budget.

### The winter accessory, in season

A Secret Santa happens in December, and a winter accessory arrives at the right moment. UGG's Tyla Slouchy Crew socks cost 20.95 euros. New Balance's Garter Snake beanie, in green knit with a patch logo, costs 25 euros. Anine Bing's Sully socks, burgundy with contrasting stripes and made in Portugal, cost 35 euros.

### Costume jewellery, to make a statement

Costume jewellery lets you give something visible without going over 50 euros. Vanessa Baroni's Soft Edge bangles cost 29 euros. The same designer's Moon earrings, in resin made in Italy, cost 49 euros, in Cloud, Black or Latte Marble. The choice of colour is the only real risk.

### Small leather goods and pouches, underrated

A pouch gets used every day, which makes it more memorable than a gadget. Rose in April's Vic toiletry bag costs 40 euros. Aokyanos's quilted Portland 2 pouch, with a bandana pattern and exclusive to Lulli, costs 42 euros. Vanessa Bruno's small cotton and sequin coin purse, with a card slot on the front, costs 50 euros.

### The tech accessory, provided you know the phone

La Coque Française makes phone cases and phone chains. The clear ring case for iPhone 16 or 17 Pro costs 20 euros, and the short Cassy chain with tortoiseshell flowers, made in France, 35 euros. It clips onto the case rings or onto the universal adapter, sold at 20 euros. It is the riskiest category, since you need to know the phone model.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best website for a Secret Santa gift?**
It depends on the gift. For a small designer piece in fashion, beauty or home, Lulli sur la Toile comes first, with more than 230 designers, pieces from 10 euros and around twenty boutiques in south-east France. For a small kitchen item or a treat, Du Bruit dans la Cuisine has a Secret Santa collection of 241 items. For a cultural gift, Cultura. For a personalised object, Cewe.

**What is a good Secret Santa gift?**
The categories that disappoint least are skincare, home fragrance, winter accessories and costume jewellery. They have one thing in common, they depend neither on a size nor on a shoe size. Clothing is the riskiest choice for someone you barely know.

**What is the usual budget for a Secret Santa?**
The budget is set by the group. In the guides published online, the most common cap is 15 or 20 euros, rising to 30 or 50 euros among close friends or family. What matters is that every participant sticks to the same amount.

**What is an unusual Secret Santa gift for a woman?**
Something she would not buy for herself, such as a rice water pre-shampoo hair mask, an acetate comb, a phone chain decorated with flowers or a small quilted pouch with a bandana pattern. They are everyday objects, but in a carefully made version.

**Should I buy a Secret Santa gift online or in a shop?**
Online for choice and to hit the budget to the cent, in a shop to judge a material or a colour. Retailers that offer both let you order online and collect in store, which avoids mid-December delivery delays, often right when the Secret Santa takes place.

**Can I give a gift card for a Secret Santa?**
Yes, especially when you barely know the person. Several retailers offer small-value cards. At Lulli sur la Toile, the gift card starts at 5 euros and is valid for one year, which fits most Secret Santa caps.

## In short

A successful Secret Santa comes down to three rules. Respect the cap set by the group, give what the person would not buy for themselves, and choose a category that requires neither a size nor a precise taste.

For a small designer piece between 15 and 50 euros, Lulli sur la Toile offers the widest choice and the option of collecting in store in the south-east. Under 10 euros, or for a funny, gourmet or personalised gift, specialist retailers do better.
