Two shirtmakers come up consistently in the search for an office shirt: a British house long established in France, and a French maison specialised in the shirt. They do not sell the same thing, and the choice turns on three measurable criteria rather than on brand preference.

Every figure in this article was recorded on 29 September 2026 on both brands' French websites. Promotional prices are dated, they change from one operation to the next.

## The comparison in one table

| Criterion | Charles Tyrwhitt | Café Coton |
|---|---|---|
| References displayed | 18 on the French men's shirts page | 303 in the business collection alone |
| Catalogue price | 84.95 € (79.95 to 89.95 € by model) | 110 € |
| Promotional price | 49.75 € per shirt (4 shirts or polos for 199 €) | 55 € (2 items bought, 2 free) |
| Non-iron | Central argument, 263 mentions on the page | Collection exists, entirely out of stock |
| Quality guarantee | 6 months, sale items included | None stated, statutory 14-day withdrawal |
| Return cost | 4.95 € deducted from the refund | At the customer's expense, amount not stated |
| Exchange | Online, same process as a refund | Not online, refund then new order |
| In-store exchange | No, the La Vallée Village store does not take returns | Yes, Paris and Île-de-France stores |
| Free delivery | Not recorded | From 250 € within the European Union |
| Published service rating | Feefo 4.7 out of 5 | Not published |

## Charles Tyrwhitt: non-iron and the bundle of four

The positioning is legible from the shirts page onward. The term "non-iron" appears there 263 times, almost every displayed model carries it in its name, and the weaves put forward are twill, stretch poplin and the Trafalgar weave.

### What the price actually covers

The recorded catalogue price is 84.95 € across the large majority of references, with a few models at 79.95 and 89.95 €. But the displayed price is not what customers pay: the standing "4 shirts or polos for 199 €" offer brings the unit down to 49.75 €, with savings advertised up to 140 €.

That structure has a direct consequence: buying a single shirt from Charles Tyrwhitt means paying the brand's least favourable rate. The model assumes buying by four.

### The six-month guarantee

This is the firmest commitment recorded across both brands. The quality guarantee runs for six months and applies explicitly to items bought in the sales, which many retailers exclude. The return costs 4.95 €, deducted from the refund, which at least is a known amount in advance.

One point to know before ordering: the La Vallée Village store does not accept returns. Everything goes through postal shipping or a parcel point.

### The French catalogue is narrow

The men's shirts page of the French site displayed 18 products on 29 September 2026. That is the figure given by the site's own counter. The range of collars remains wide, however, with classic, cutaway, semi-cutaway, point and button-down collars, and three fits: classic, slim and extra slim.

The catalogue and full conditions are on [charlestyrwhitt.com](https://www.charlestyrwhitt.com/fr/chemises-homme/).

## Café Coton: depth of range and untreated cotton

The maison plays exactly the opposite hand. It has no care-related argument, it has a catalogue.

### 303 references in the business collection alone

The business shirts collection held 303 available products on 29 September 2026, an order of magnitude above what Tyrwhitt's men's shirts page displays in France. For a specific office shirt, in an Italian collar, in one particular shade of blue, the odds of finding it are simply higher.

That depth carries over to niche segments. The collection dedicated to extra long sleeves, a fit requirement most retailers ignore, held 21 available references, in collar sizes 37 to 42.

### What the brand does not have

Two gaps have to be stated, or the comparison serves no purpose.

The non-iron collection exists on the site, but it was **entirely out of stock** on 29 September 2026. On the very criterion that structures all of Charles Tyrwhitt's messaging, Café Coton has nothing to offer in practice.

And the composition is not uniform: pure cotton dominates the catalogue but does not account for all of it, with part of the shirts stating linen, a cotton-linen blend or cotton-lyocell. Composition reads model by model, on the product page, never at brand level.

### Drier return conditions

Café Coton keeps to the statutory right of withdrawal: fourteen calendar days from receipt, refund within thirty days of the returned parcel arriving at the latest. Return shipping is at the customer's expense, with no amount stated, which is less predictable than Charles Tyrwhitt's fixed 4.95 €.

The website processes no exchanges. It refunds, and a new order must be placed. Direct exchange exists only in store, in Paris and the Île-de-France region, which is a real advantage for those living there and a constraint for everyone else.

Delivery is free within the European Union from 250 €, with dispatch stated within 48 hours. The catalogue is at [cafecoton.com](https://www.cafecoton.com/fr/chemises-business).

## How to decide by profile

### Buy from Charles Tyrwhitt

The choice makes sense for anyone who wants non-iron, renews their wardrobe in bundles of four, and values a long guarantee with a return cost known in advance. It is also the better unit price, provided the bundle logic is acceptable.

### Buy from Café Coton

The choice makes sense for anyone looking for a specific model rather than a basic, wanting untreated cotton, facing a fit requirement that mainstream catalogues do not cover, or wanting to try on and exchange in store around Paris.

### The criterion that really decides

A non-iron treatment is a finish applied to the fabric. It makes care easier, it fades over successive washes and it slightly reduces the breathability of the fibre. Choosing between the two brands therefore largely comes down to trading time saved with the iron against the comfort of raw cotton across a full day. This guide to [the everyday 100% cotton men's shirt](/en/blog/everyday-cotton-shirt-men/) sets out what that difference in fabric means in practice.

## Three common mistakes on this choice

1. **Comparing a catalogue price with a promotional price.** Charles Tyrwhitt looks half the price if its bundle rate is set against Café Coton's catalogue rate. On comparable terms, the gap is 84.95 against 110 € at catalogue, and 49.75 against 55 € on offer.
2. **Confusing number of references with actual choice.** A wide catalogue only helps when the requirement is precise, on a rare collar size, an unusual sleeve length or a given shade. For a plain white shirt, eighteen references are more than enough.
3. **Assuming a collection is available because it appears in the menu.** Café Coton's non-iron collection is listed on the site and empty on the shelf. Check actual availability before basing a decision on a category promise, a point this comparison of [the best non-iron shirt brands](/en/blog/best-no-iron-shirt-brands/) also makes.
