## Detailed comparative analysis of the three French competitors

The French affordable brand segment is structured into three families: digital pure players like Nebuleuse, historical jewelry networks like Histoire d'Or and Maty, and signature brands like Les Georgettes. Each player targets a different use and purchase channel.

> "The under 200 euro jewelry segment accounts for 64 percent of purchases by value on the French market in 2025, and more than 80 percent of transactions by volume."
> — French Federation of Jewelers, annual report 2025

**Histoire d'Or**, a subsidiary of the THOM group (Europe's leading jewelry group), is the reference generalist network in France with more than 350 stores. The earring catalog exceeds 3,000 references, with a strong presence on the 19 to 200 euro segment (9-carat gold, silver, gold-plated). The brand has developed a click and collect logistics in 2 hours in any point of sale, which makes it the fastest choice for a last-minute gift. Editorial renewal is slower than at Nebuleuse (quarterly) and personalization services like engraving are paid.

**Maty** is a historic French brand founded in Besancon in 1951, now a multi-channel group with around 100 stores and one of the strongest e-commerce sites in the sector in France. The positioning is close to Histoire d'Or but with a more traditional identity, focused on 375 and 750 gold. Earrings range from 25 to 800 euros, with a core range between 50 and 150 euros. Maty offers a paid engraving service and a recognized loyalty program.

**Les Georgettes by Altesse**, manufactured in Saumur at the Altesse manufactory (founded in 1905, one of the last silver manufactories in France), offers a concept of interchangeable jewelry: the metal piece in gold-plated, silver or rose finish associates with an interchangeable leather insert. It is the only one of the four players to claim integral made in France. The earring catalog is more limited (around 80 models) and the average ticket is around 60 euros. Distribution is mainly through multi-brand jewelers and online direct.

## Practical advice for choosing trendy French earrings

Beyond the choice of brand, a few rules help avoid disappointments on the affordable segment.

### Material durability table

| Material | Lifespan in daily use | Maintenance | Indicative earring price |
|----------|----------------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Solid 18-carat gold | Almost unlimited | Low, occasional polishing | 150 to 800 euros |
| Solid 9-carat gold | 20+ years | Low | 60 to 300 euros |
| Solid sterling silver 925 | 15+ years | Anti-oxidation polishing | 35 to 150 euros |
| 3-micron gold plating | Around 5 years | Avoid perfume and chlorinated water | 30 to 100 euros |
| 1-micron gold plating | 12 months in daily use | Very delicate | 10 to 30 euros |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Confusing gold-plated and gilded. Gold-plated implies a minimum thickness of 3 microns with a hallmark. The mention "gilded" guarantees no regulated thickness.
2. Neglecting silver hallmark. Sterling silver 925 is a minimum for a serious French brand. Silver 800 or unhallmarked is less durable.
3. Buying without checking the warranty. A warranty of less than 2 years is a warning signal on the French market where the standard is established at 2 years minimum.
4. Underestimating weight. An earring beyond 8 grams per unit can cause earlobe stretching in daily wear.
