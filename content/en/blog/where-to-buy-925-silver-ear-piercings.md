## Why choose 925 silver for an ear piercing

**925 silver**, also called sterling silver, is an alloy made of 92.5 percent pure silver and 7.5 percent of other metals, most often copper. This composition is the international standard for silver jewelry. It strikes a balance between metal purity and the strength needed for daily wear.

For an ear piercing, **925 silver** offers three concrete advantages. Its lifespan far exceeds that of low-grade surgical steel. Its surface polishes easily, allowing maintenance with a simple cloth. Finally, its skin compatibility is good for 90 to 95 percent of adult wearers, according to figures from the French Federation HBJO cited by BJOP in 2024.

**925 silver** is not, however, the best choice for a piercing in the healing phase. During the first 6 to 12 weeks, G23 implant grade titanium remains recommended by most professional piercers. Once the piercing has healed, switching to **925 silver** poses no issue for most ear morphologies.

## Practical tips for buying 925 silver ear piercings

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying a **925 silver** piercing for initial healing: prefer G23 titanium for 6 to 12 weeks before switching to silver.
2. Ignoring the hallmark: any genuine **925 silver** piece bears a legal hallmark (Minerva head in France, 925 on international markets).
3. Forgetting the return policy: on online purchases, check that the shop applies 14 to 30 days of free returns before ordering.
