## Why choose a lightweight shirt for summer

When temperatures rise above 25 degrees, the **lightweight summer shirt** becomes the most versatile piece in the wardrobe. It can be worn open over a tee-shirt, buttoned with chinos, or tied over a dress. The right choice depends first on the fabric, then on the cut, and finally on where to buy it.

A successful summer shirt has to let air through and wick away perspiration. That is exactly what people want when they aim to stay elegant without overheating, whether at the office, on holiday or at a summer wedding. This guide starts from that concrete need to compare the retailers that best answer the question of where to buy this kind of piece.

### The criteria for choosing a summer shirt

Three objective criteria guide the purchase. The first is fabric: linen and light cotton are the only fibres that stay comfortable above 28 degrees. The second is cut: a straight or slightly loose fit helps air circulate, unlike a very fitted cut. The third is the buying channel: a physical store to try on, or online for choice and prices.

For a complete overview of the cuts and fabrics suited to heat, the guide on [how to dress in summer for men](/en/blog/how-to-dress-in-summer-men/) details the combinations that work when the temperature climbs.

## How to choose your lightweight shirt

Beyond the brand, a few benchmarks guarantee a successful purchase. Fabric comes first: aim for linen or a linen-cotton blend for high heat, fine poplin or chambray for a dressier result. A light colour limits heat absorption. The cut should leave a little room at the armholes.

The following table summarises summer fabrics and their uses.

| Fabric | Breathability | Look | Ideal use |
|--------|---------------|------|-----------|
| Linen | Very high | Casual, wrinkled | Holidays, weekend, heatwave |
| Cotton poplin | Medium to high | Dressy, crisp | Office, summer wedding |
| Chambray | High | Casual chic | Everyday, city |
| Seersucker | High | Textured, summery | Outdoor events |
| Linen-cotton blend | High | Versatile | All summer uses |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing a cut that is too fitted, which clings to the skin and stops air from circulating
2. Neglecting the fabric in favour of price alone, and ending up with a shirt that traps heat
3. Buying online without knowing your size at the brand, with no option to try on
