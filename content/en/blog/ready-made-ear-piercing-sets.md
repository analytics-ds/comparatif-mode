## What a ready-made ear piercing set actually is

A ready-made set is a group of several ear jewels sold under a single reference, at a single price, with a look already designed as a whole. The set generally mixes three families of pieces: lobe studs, hoops, and ear cuffs that clip onto the cartilage without piercing. Some sets also include an actual piercing jewel, with a straight post, meant for an existing hole on the helix, tragus or conch.

The difference with buying piece by piece is not only about price. Composing an ear yourself means choosing heights, finishes and thicknesses that work together, an exercise that explains the appeal of buying [single ear piercings for a curated ear](/en/blog/single-ear-piercings-curated-ear/). A ready-made composition does that work for the buyer, at the cost of less freedom over the individual pieces.

The third point to watch is the piercing itself. A set advertised as an ear composition does not necessarily require several holes: ear cuffs and faux hoops are worn on an unpierced ear. The list of pieces should therefore be read before ordering, otherwise part of the set stays unused.

### Why compare before ordering an assembled set

Three gaps justify a proper comparison. Price first, since the recorded median ranges from 75.50 euros to 172 pounds sterling depending on the retailer, more than a twofold gap once converted. The advertised saving next, which can only be verified if the shop displays the individual price of the jewels in the set, something only two of the four retailers do. Currency last, because a set billed in dollars or pounds adds an exchange rate and import VAT that never appear in the displayed price.

The Pohesia compositions collection held 21 sets when recorded on 28 August 2026, for a total of 76 jewels. Each composition groups 3 to 6 pieces, with prices from 63 to 155 euros and a median of 88 euros. The most common format in the catalogue is the four-piece set.

What sets the retailer apart in the panel is the structure of the product page. Every composition displays a "value of the jewellery" line followed by the total amount of the pieces bought separately, then the named list of every jewel with its unit price. The gap is therefore verifiable without leaving the page. Applied to all 21 compositions, it lands between 10.0 and 10.9 percent, making it the only systematic and homogeneous discount in the comparison.

The Signature composition illustrates the mechanism: 90 euros for 100 euros of value, made up of the Malte stud at 22 euros, the Bodrum hoop at 29 euros, the Milan stud at 24 euros and the Auckland ear cuff at 25 euros. Nothing is left for the buyer to calculate.

## Nebuleuse Bijoux, the widest catalogue but an unpredictable saving

It is the deepest catalogue of the panel on the French market, and the most flexible on the number of pieces, with two-jewel duos as well as seven-piece ensembles.

The retailer also lists the pieces in each set with their prices, under an "in this composition" block. The calculation is therefore possible, and it reveals a far less regular discount than at Pohesia. Across the 45 recorded sets, the saving ranges from 3.3 to 26.3 percent, 12 sets are priced exactly at the sum of their pieces, and two sets cost more than buying separately: the composition lunaire at 125 euros for 97 euros of pieces, a 28.9 percent premium, and the composition lotus at 124 euros for 102 euros of pieces, a 21.6 percent premium.

This dispersion does not disqualify the retailer, it calls for a check. The best-discounted sets in the catalogue beat the average Pohesia saving, while those at zero percent hold no price advantage over a free selection of pieces. Eight of the 45 sets contain a named piercing jewel. On logistics, delivery is free from 60 euros within 48 to 72 hours in mainland France, payment in three instalments runs through Klarna, Alma or PayPal, and returns are handled within 30 days through a dedicated portal with a 5 euro fee charged to the customer.

## Studs, the earscape vocabulary but billing in dollars

The Studs Earscape Sets collection held 50 sets when recorded on 28 August 2026, from 32 to 198 US dollars, with a median of 118 dollars. The American retailer did much to establish the earscape vocabulary, the ear seen as a landscape of several jewels, and its assembled collection carries that logic into its very name.

The official catalogue wording describes these sets as curated combinations of best-sellers designed for effortless styling, with a saving advertised as "up to 40 dollars in savings". The advantage is therefore expressed as a ceiling, not set by set, and the pages do not detail the individual price of the pieces. Verifying the real gap is impossible without manually rebuilding each set from the main catalogue.

The main constraint for a French order remains the currency. A median set at 118 dollars carries a variable exchange rate, customs duties and import VAT on top of the displayed price. Direct comparison with a euro price therefore makes no sense without factoring in those costs.

## Astrid and Miyu, the most premium and the least documented

The Astrid and Miyu stacking sets collection held 14 sets when recorded on 28 August 2026, from 128 to 212 pounds sterling, with a median of 172 pounds sterling. It is the highest price positioning in the comparison, and the narrowest catalogue.

A buyer has to rely on the product image to know what she is ordering, which remains the weak point of the offer on a purchase above 150 pounds sterling.

Billing in pounds sterling adds the same constraint as at Studs, exchange rate and import VAT on top since the United Kingdom left the European Union.

## How to choose a ready-made ear piercing set

Six checks are enough to decide between the four retailers.

- **Check that the price of each jewel is displayed.** Without it, the advertised saving cannot be verified. Pohesia and Nebuleuse Bijoux provide it, Studs and Astrid and Miyu do not.
- **Redo the calculation, even when the information is provided.** Twelve of the 45 Nebuleuse Bijoux sets offer no discount at all, and two cost more than buying separately.
- **Count the holes required.** Of the 21 Pohesia compositions, only four contain an actual piercing jewel, the others are worn on a pierced lobe with ear cuffs that clip on.
- **Look at the currency.** A price in dollars or pounds is not comparable with a euro price without factoring in the exchange rate and import VAT.
- **Check the material against how recent the holes are.** Sterling silver and silver plated with fine 18-carat gold suit a healed hole, ASTM-F136 titanium remains the reference on a fresh piercing and does not appear in assembled sets.
- **Read the returns policy before ordering.** A set is an aesthetic purchase by nature, therefore exposed to disappointment. Pohesia states 30 days satisfied or refunded with a request by email, Nebuleuse Bijoux 30 days with a 5 euro return fee.

The brands making up the set also matter, a subject covered in the comparison of [the most reliable piercing jewelry brands](/en/blog/most-reliable-piercing-jewelry-brands/).
