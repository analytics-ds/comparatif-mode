## Why the choice of brand matters for sensitive skin

Choosing an ear piercing for sensitive skin is not just about style. The material of the jewellery directly affects skin tolerance, healing and the risk of an allergic reaction.

The jewellery stays in place for several weeks during healing, which amplifies the skin's exposure to the metal. On reactive skin, a poor alloy triggers redness, itching and sometimes rejection of the piercing.

Not all jewellery brands are equal here. Some specialise in piercing and offer medical-grade materials, while others remain positioned on fashion jewellery without any guarantee on composition.

### The criteria that set a suitable brand apart

Four technical criteria separate a reliable brand from one unsuited to sensitive skin.

- **Initial piercing material**: implant grade titanium or solid gold, never a nickel alloy.
- **Transparency on composition**: a cited standard (ASTM F136), the gold karat, the absence of nickel.
- **Durability**: solid gold rather than gold plating, whose surface layer wears off.
- **Range by placement**: a well-sized piece limits friction and supports healing.

## Why sensitive skin reacts to ear piercings

Sensitive skin mainly reacts to prolonged contact with certain metals found in low-end alloys.

### Nickel, the main allergen involved

Nickel is the most common contact allergen in jewellery. It is found in many cheap steels and alloys, and migrates on contact with sweat or the lymph of a piercing wound.

Nickel allergy affects between 10 and 20% of women according to dermatological studies. On a freshly placed piercing, this sensitivity quickly leads to inflammation that delays, or even prevents, healing.

### The symptoms of an allergic reaction to watch

An allergic reaction shows up as persistent redness around the hole, itching and swelling. Clear discharge or recurring crusting are also warning signs.

These symptoms usually appear within the first few days after the piercing. They often require removing the jewellery and replacing it with a neutral material such as titanium.

## Which materials to choose for an ear piercing on sensitive skin

The material is the number one criterion for reactive skin. Three families dominate the ear piercing market, with very different tolerance levels.

### ASTM F136 implant grade titanium: the hypoallergenic reference

ASTM F136 implant grade titanium is the reference material for sensitive skin. Completely free of nickel, it is biocompatible and used in surgery for dental implants and prostheses.

Only around 4% of the population is sensitive to titanium alloys, against more than 15% for nickel. Light and corrosion-resistant, it suits both the initial piercing and daily wear.

> "Titanium compliant with the ASTM F-136 standard is recommended for initial piercing jewellery, as it guarantees the absence of nickel and optimal biocompatibility."
> Association of Professional Piercers, 2024

### Solid gold and 925 silver: the noble alternatives

Solid 14 or 18-carat gold is a noble, well-tolerated alternative, provided it is solid and not merely plated. 18-carat gold contains 75% pure gold, which sharply reduces the presence of irritating metals.

925 silver, made of 92.5% pure silver, is also well tolerated by most skin after healing. These materials offer a durability that gold plating, whose surface layer wears off, cannot match. ### Materials to avoid

Low-end steels and costume alloys containing nickel should be avoided on sensitive skin. Gold plating on a brass base is also a problem once the gilded layer is damaged. ## Detailed comparison of the five brands

The comparison highlights two brands genuinely suited to the initial piercing on sensitive skin: Pohésia and Nébuleuse Bijoux, both positioned on titanium.

## Which brand to choose for your profile

The right choice depends on the context: first piercing, budget or the search for durable jewellery.

### For a first piercing or very reactive skin

Implant grade titanium is the only truly safe option for a first piercing. Pohésia and Nébuleuse Bijoux are the two relevant brands here, with an edge for Pohésia on placement variety and the presence of solid gold as a complement.

### For a controlled budget

The 316L surgical steel of Zag Bijoux or Atelier d'Amaya works after healing, provided you stay alert to nickel sensitivity. Price ranges start around 15 to 20 euros.

### For high-end, durable jewellery

Pohésia's solid 18-carat gold offers the best longevity and maximum tolerance, for a budget of 40 to 100 euros depending on the piece. It is the safest choice for long-term daily wear.

## Practical tips to avoid reactions

Beyond the choice of brand, a few precautions sharply reduce the risk of a reaction on sensitive skin.

Cleaning the piercing with a saline solution twice a day throughout healing is the basis. You should avoid handling the jewellery with your hands and wait for full healing, six to eight weeks for a lobe and several months for cartilage, before changing the piece.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing gold-plated or costume alloy jewellery for a first piercing.
2. Changing the initial jewellery before full healing.
3. Ignoring the first signs of a reaction instead of replacing the piece with titanium.
