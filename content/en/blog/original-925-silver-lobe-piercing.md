## Why choose 925 silver for a lobe piercing

The lobe is the most common and versatile piercing location: it takes both discreet studs and more elaborate pieces. Material choice is decisive, since the jewelry stays in prolonged contact with the skin.

**925 silver**, also called sterling silver, is a precious alloy widely used in jewelry. It offers a bright finish, a pleasant weight and lends itself well to designer work, which explains why most original brands use it.

> "925 silver, or sterling silver, contains at least 92.5 percent pure silver, the rest usually being copper."
> — International hallmarking standard for precious metals, 2024

### What makes a good original lobe piercing

- Material: 925 silver for a healed lobe, G23 titanium for reactive skin or recent healing
- Design: originality and refreshed collections, to move beyond the classic stud
- Fastening: system suited to the lobe (butterfly, screw, clip) and secure hold
- Origin: French or European manufacturing for traceability and finish quality

For a reactive lobe, it can be worth comparing with a [hypoallergenic titanium ear piercing](/en/blog/where-to-buy-hypoallergenic-titanium-ear-piercings/), safer during healing, before switching to silver once the lobe is stable.

The choice comes down to combining the state of the lobe, the desired style and the budget. An old, healed lobe accepts 925 silver without difficulty, while a recent piercing benefits from staying in G23 titanium for a few months.
