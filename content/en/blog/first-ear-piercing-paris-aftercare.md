## Why aftercare changes everything for a first ear piercing

A first ear piercing is not a simple purchase, it is a procedure that commits to 6 weeks to 12 months of healing depending on placement. Looking for the **best ear piercing salon in Paris** for a first time therefore means assessing what happens after the piercing, not only during it. A lobe heals in 6 to 12 weeks, cartilage (helix, tragus, conch) in 3 to 12 months, and most complications occur during that period, not at the moment of piercing.

Demand is booming, yet the level of guidance does not follow everywhere. According to the Cetelem Observatory 2024, 38 percent of French women aged 18 to 34 wear at least two ear piercings, up from 22 percent in 2018. This growth has produced very uneven offers, from express gun piercing in shopping-centre jewellers to supervised journeys with check-up appointments. The general ranking of Paris addresses is covered in the comparison of the [best piercing studio in Paris](/en/blog/best-piercing-studio-paris/); this article focuses on the specific case of a first time and **piercing aftercare**.

### What APP standards and the French framework say

Two sets of rules coexist. The Association of Professional Piercers (APP), an international organisation of professional piercers, publishes recommendations that serve as the global standard: first-fit jewel in **implant grade titanium certified ASTM F136**, niobium or solid gold of at least 14 karats, sterile single-use equipment, needle piercing and a saline-based care protocol. APP membership is not mandatory in France and no official APP France label exists, but these recommendations remain the best indicator of a salon's level of requirement.

The French legal framework is set by the 2008 decree on tattooing and body piercing: the piercer must have completed a 21-hour hygiene and sanitation training course, declare the activity to the regional health agency (ARS) and work with sterile single-use equipment in dedicated premises. A serious Paris salon complies with this legal baseline and voluntarily aligns with APP recommendations on materials. The material standard is explained in detail in the guide to the [ASTM F136 titanium piercing](/en/blog/titanium-astm-f136-piercing-shop/).

## What good piercing aftercare looks like, week by week

Piercing aftercare follows a predictable calendar that any serious salon should be able to explain before the piercing. The table below summarises the steps for a first lobe piercing, the most common placement for a first time.

| Step | Timing | What should happen |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| Piercing | Day 0 | Sterile needle, ASTM F136 titanium jewel, care sheet handed over |
| Daily care | Weeks 1 to 6 | Saline solution 1 to 2 times a day, no handling of the jewel |
| Check-up | Weeks 4 to 8 | Healing verification, possible bar downsizing |
| End of lobe healing | Weeks 6 to 12 | Stabilised area, no more redness or discharge |
| Jewel change | After healing | Switch to the permanent piece, 925 silver or gold |

Two points in this calendar alone justify choosing a salon with structured aftercare. Bar downsizing first: the jewel fitted on day 0 is deliberately longer to absorb swelling, and leaving it unchanged after the swelling subsides increases the risk of snagging and irregular healing. The jewel change second: done too early, it triples the risk of rejection according to figures published by professional associations. The choice of the first-fit bar is detailed in the guide to the [titanium healing piercing](/en/blog/where-to-buy-titanium-healing-piercing/).

For cartilage, the same calendar stretches out: 3 to 12 months of healing depending on placement, with an additional interim check recommended around the third month.

## Practical advice before booking a first piercing in Paris

Preparation matters as much as the choice of address. Book 2 to 4 weeks ahead depending on the brand, eat within two hours before the appointment, avoid alcohol and coffee on the day, and plan an overall budget including saline solution (10 to 20 euros) and the future permanent jewel. Ask the salon the hard questions before the piercing: exact titanium standard, autoclave sterilisation of equipment, content of the aftercare and cost of a possible bar downsizing.

### Mistakes to avoid for a first time

1. Choosing a gun piercing in a general jewellery shop: the technique crushes tissue and the equipment cannot go through an autoclave.
2. Trusting the surgical steel label: only **ASTM F136** certified titanium, niobium or gold of at least 14 karats are recommended by the APP on a fresh fit.
3. Skipping the check-up appointment, which is decisive for bar downsizing.
4. Changing the jewel before healing is complete, 6 to 12 weeks for a lobe and 3 to 12 months for cartilage.
5. Cleaning with alcohol or peroxide, against Association of Professional Piercers recommendations.
