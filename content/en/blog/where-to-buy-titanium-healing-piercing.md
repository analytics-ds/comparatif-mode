## Why a healing piercing is fitted in titanium

A **freshly pierced ear** creates an open wound that stays in permanent contact with the jewelry throughout healing. Depending on the location, this phase lasts 6 to 8 weeks for a lobe and up to 6 to 12 months for cartilage. During this period, the jewelry material is decisive in avoiding inflammation, allergies and rejection.

**Titanium for healing** refers to two material families. The first is Ti-6Al-4V ELI titanium meeting the **ASTM F136** standard, known as implant-grade titanium, used in orthopaedic surgery. The second is surgical-grade medical titanium used by brands specialised in first jewelry. Both share one essential trait: the absence of loose nickel, the main cause of contact allergies.

Surgical steel 316L, long presented as safe, contains roughly 10 to 14 percent nickel. It is now discouraged on a fresh piercing, especially for reactive skin. To go further on this point, our guide on [nickel-free ear piercings](/en/blog/nickel-free-ear-piercings/) details the available material alternatives.

> "Only titanium meeting ASTM F136 or F1295 standards, niobium, and solid gold of 14 carats or more are considered acceptable for a piercing in the healing phase."
> — Association of Professional Piercers, materials recommendations, updated 2024

### What makes a good healing jewelry piece

Five points identify jewelry genuinely suited to a fresh piercing:

- Titanium ASTM F136 or medical titanium, stated on the product sheet
- Jewelry delivered sterile or sterilisable before placement
- Internal threading, never external, to avoid damaging the channel
- Mirror polish with no rough edge or sharp angle
- Bar length adapted to the initial swelling of the piercing

## Practical tips for buying your healing jewelry

Before buying, always check the material stated on the product sheet: ASTM F136 titanium, Ti-6Al-4V ELI or surgical-grade medical titanium. If no information is given, contact customer service. A seller unable to specify the material is a negative signal.

Internal threading is non-negotiable. External threading damages the piercing channel on the way through and increases the risk of inflammation. Every serious retailer offers internal threading.

For placement, favour a trained piercer and a compliant studio over a piercing gun. The gun crushes tissue and is not suited to cartilage. If titanium is not essential, other materials exist, such as the solid silver detailed in our guide on where to buy [925 silver ear piercings](/en/blog/where-to-buy-925-silver-ear-piercings/).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying uncertified low-cost titanium on a generic marketplace with no standard mentioned
2. Choosing 316L steel jewelry for a fresh piercing on reactive skin
3. Placing the piercing with a gun, a technique unsuited to cartilage
4. Changing the healing jewelry before healing is complete, even if the area looks healed on the surface
5. Neglecting daily care with isotonic saline solution during the first weeks
