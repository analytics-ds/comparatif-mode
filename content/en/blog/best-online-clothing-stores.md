Buying clothes online has become a reflex for millions of consumers in France. Time saving, wider choice, the ability to compare prices in just a few clicks: the advantages are numerous. But faced with the growing number of platforms, finding the **best online clothing site** for your needs is not always straightforward. This comparison reviews the main players in the market, their strengths and the criteria to consider before placing an order.

## Comparison table of the best online clothing sites

| Site | Type | Strength | Ideal profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zalando** | Multi-brand marketplace | Very wide catalogue, easy returns | Versatile, all budgets |
| **ASOS** | Multi-brand + own label | Trending fashion for 20-35s, frequent updates | Fashion-conscious, mid budget |

## The essential generalist platforms

### Zalando

Zalando is the European leader in fashion e-commerce. Its main strength lies in the depth of its catalogue: sportswear, casual, formal, major brands and emerging labels coexist on a single platform. The return policy is one of the most flexible on the market, with an extended window that allows ordering without pressure and returning anything that does not fit. Delivery is fast, and the site regularly offers significant promotional deals. The downside: the sheer volume of products can make browsing tedious without precise filters.

For those looking for specific brands, our guide to the [best designer women's clothing websites](/en/blog/best-websites-designer-womens-clothing/) is a useful complement to this selection.

### ASOS

ASOS primarily targets the 20-35 age group with an offer that mixes hundreds of brands with its own lines. The catalogue is updated very frequently, making it a go-to for following trends without spending a fortune. The site features a size guide and displays customer reviews, which help anticipate fit issues. Standard delivery is generally free above a minimum spend, and returns are possible within a reasonable period. ASOS sizing can sometimes lack reference points for non-standard body types.

### La Redoute

La Redoute combines fashion, home and children's products in a single platform, making it a practical choice for families. One of its features is facilitated payment, often interest-free, allowing purchases to be spread over time. Its own-brand selection sits alongside external brands, and sales are regularly generous. Navigation is well structured by product category and occasion. Stock turnover is slower than ASOS for trending women's fashion.

## French fashion sites with a strong identity

### Sézane

Sézane has built a solid reputation around a clear positioning: Parisian-inspired clothing, carefully chosen fabrics, limited-edition collections. The brand operates exclusively online (with a few occasional physical spaces), which keeps prices in the premium segment without reaching luxury territory. Women's collections are at the heart of the offer, with a recent extension into menswear. The site is well designed and the return policy is clear. One downside: items sell out quickly when collections launch.

### Other French brands online

Brands such as Rouje, Jacquemus and Sessùn have developed effective online shops with polished shopping experiences. They share a positioning close to Sézane: French creation, defined aesthetic, mid to high price point. For shoes and accessories to complete these outfits, our selection of [best websites for trendy women's trainers online](/en/blog/best-websites-trendy-womens-trainers/) provides complementary references.

## Second-hand online: a serious alternative

### Vinted

Vinted has become the first port of call for buying second-hand clothes in France. The model is simple: individuals sell their items to other individuals, and the platform handles payment security and buyer protection. Prices are significantly below retail, and it is possible to find branded pieces in excellent condition at a fraction of their original price. The search function works well with filters for size, brand, condition and price.

The main drawback: no returns possible (except in the event of a proven dispute). Items must therefore be read carefully, with close attention to descriptions and photos before confirming.

### Vide-dressing and Depop

Other platforms such as Vide-dressing or Depop target slightly different audiences. Vide-dressing focuses on branded items and organised consignment, while Depop attracts more streetwear and vintage profiles. Both are worth exploring if Vinted does not have exactly what you are looking for.

## Buying clothes online on a tight budget

For purchases with a limited budget, two options dominate.

**Kiabi** is a long-established player in affordable ready-to-wear in France. The site covers all age groups and regularly runs promotions. Quality is consistent with the price points offered, and the widespread physical presence (numerous stores) makes exchanges and returns straightforward.

**Shein** is the platform that has most disrupted the market in recent years with particularly low prices. It represents a significant share of online clothing spending by volume. Delivery times can be longer than European sites, and quality varies by item. This option suits occasional seasonal purchases rather than building a lasting wardrobe.

## Five criteria for choosing your online clothing site

**1. The return policy.** This is probably the most important criterion for shopping with peace of mind. Free or low-cost returns, with a sufficient window, completely changes the shopping experience. Check whether sale items are covered.

**2. The size guide.** A good size guide, complemented by customer reviews, considerably reduces the risk of a failed order. Some sites incorporate body-shape tools or size suggestions based on your measurements. For choosing the right cut based on your silhouette, our guide on [which jeans suit which body shape](/en/blog/which-jeans-for-body-shape/) illustrates the importance of this criterion well.

**3. Delivery charges.** Compare free delivery thresholds and lead times. A site that is cheaper per item may end up costing more if shipping fees are added to each order.

**4. Variety of offer.** Are you looking for a specific brand, or do you prefer browsing a wide catalogue? Marketplaces (Zalando, ASOS) give access to hundreds of brands, while own-brand sites (Sézane, Rouje) offer a consistent identity but a more limited choice.

**5. Reliability of customer reviews.** Reviews on fit, fabric quality and customer service are invaluable. Favour platforms that display verified and recent reviews rather than overall scores with no context.
