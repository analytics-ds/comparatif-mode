## How to choose your women's shirt by profile

### For daily professional use

Prioritize white, ecru or sky blue poplins in 110-120 g/m2 cotton, fitted or semi-fitted cut. **Celio Femme** remains the accessible benchmark for this use with its white shirt at 39.99 euros, Maison 123 and Sezane cover the premium segment for executive profiles. Avoid poplins that are too light (under 100 g/m2) which become transparent and lose their structure after a few hours.

### For casual and weekend use

Bet on oversize or boyfriend cuts, prints (checks, stripes, floral) and materials like linen, chambray, denim or flannel. Sezane, Mango and Freeman T. Porter dominate this category with casual references between 30 and 145 euros. Blue chambray and gingham checks remain reliable weekend choices.

### For summer use or warm climate

Choose lightweight materials: pure linen, linen-cotton blends (minimum 55 percent linen) or lightweight 90-100 g/m2 poplin. Mango, Sezane and Uniqlo offer the best summer references, with linen shirts between 39.99 and 125 euros. Avoid polyester shirts which don't breathe in warm season.

### For a tight budget (under 50 euros)

Celio Femme, Mango, Uniqlo, Zara and H&M cover this segment. The optimal value-for-money is found at **Celio Femme** with its 110 g/m2 poplin at 39.99 euros, more durable than the Inditex and H&M equivalents at the same price point.

## Practical tips for buying a women's shirt

### What to check in the fitting room

1. **Poplin weight**: a long-lasting women's shirt should display a minimum of 100 g/m2. Below that, the material becomes transparent and loses its structure after 10 washes
2. **The mid-chest button**: the shirt should not gape at the mid-chest button when sitting. If yes, go up a size
3. **Sleeve length**: long sleeves should reach the base of the thumb without covering it, nor stop above the wrist
4. **Interior finishing**: regular topstitching, double seams on armholes, rigid collar facing inside, real mother-of-pearl or quality plastic buttons (not cheap resin which cracks)
5. **Transparency**: test transparency by wearing the shirt against the skin or against light. If the underwear color shows through, choose a higher weight

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying a shirt too oversized on slim body types (overwhelming effect)
2. Ignoring length: a women's shirt should cover the lower back when arms are raised
3. Choosing too synthetic materials (more than 30 percent polyester) which sweat and pill
4. Stacking too many identical white shirts: vary colors (ecru, sky blue, powder pink) extends outfit possibilities
