## Buying online or in a studio: what changes for a conch

The conch goes through the central part of the ear cartilage, the concha, a thick and poorly vascularized area. It takes much longer to heal than a lobe, counted in months.

This means a two-step journey:

- **The piercing, in a studio**: the piercer supplies sterile, suitable jewelry, most often a straight titanium barbell that leaves room for swelling. Jewelry for the initial piercing is not ordered online.
- **Changing jewelry, online**: once the conch has healed or with the piercer's approval, buying online opens up the choice of rings, studs and gold-tone pieces.

To find a professional, the comparison of the [best piercing studios in Paris](/en/blog/best-piercing-studio-paris/) details the hygiene and aftercare criteria to check.

### ASTM F136 titanium: the benchmark

ASTM F136 titanium, also called grade 23 or G23, is the alloy most piercers recommend. It releases no nickel and meets the standard used for surgical implants. It is the material to favor for a conch that is still reactive, as for all [hypoallergenic titanium ear piercings](/en/blog/where-to-buy-hypoallergenic-titanium-ear-piercings/).

### Nickel, the main cause of reactions

Nickel is the leading cause of contact allergy linked to jewelry in Europe. The REACH regulation (Annex XVII, entry 27) limits nickel release from jewelry inserted into a pierced part of the body to less than 0.2 microgram per cm² per week, against 0.5 for jewelry simply worn on the skin ([text of Annex XVII on the ECHA website](https://www.echa.europa.eu/documents/10162/3bbe9024-52a6-8e63-5581-e686331eb459)). This is why [nickel-free ear piercings](/en/blog/nickel-free-ear-piercings/) are favored for cartilage.

### Gold-plated 925 silver

Gold plating means a thin layer of gold applied over a base, here 925 silver. It gives a gold look at a low price, but the layer wears over time and the base stays in contact with the skin. It is best kept for a fully healed conch, as people with [sensitive skin](/en/blog/ear-piercing-sensitive-skin/) who react to silver can confirm.

## How to buy a conch piercing online without making a mistake

- Check the titanium grade stated (ASTM F136 or G23) on the product page
- Check the ring diameter (most often 10 to 14 mm for a conch) and the post thickness before ordering
- For a gold look, identify the base material (titanium or 925 silver)
- Read the site's shipping and returns policy before confirming
- Ask the piercer before replacing the initial jewelry
