## Why the choice of site matters for a conch piercing

The conch passes through the central part of the ear cartilage, the concha, a thick and poorly vascularized area. This low blood supply explains a long healing time, 6 to 12 months, compared with 6 to 8 weeks for a simple lobe.

Throughout this period, the tissue remains reactive to the jewelry material. When buying online, there is a double challenge: you need a site that clearly states the jewelry composition and offers ASTM F136 titanium, the safest material for a fresh conch.

### Nickel, the main cause of reaction

Nickel is the leading cause of contact dermatitis linked to jewelry. An estimated 10 to 20% of the population is sensitive to it, with a higher prevalence among women. Jewelry that releases nickel causes redness, itching and oozing around the conch.

The European REACH regulation frames this release for objects in prolonged contact with the skin: it must not exceed 0.5 microgram per cm² per week. Truly safe jewelry stays well below this threshold, ideally at zero nickel. This is why pieces sold as [nickel-free ear piercings](/en/blog/nickel-free-ear-piercings/) are preferred from the start, in store and online.

### Diameter, gauge and ring shape

A conch ring is most often chosen between 10 and 14 mm in diameter, depending on ear anatomy, with a standard 1.2 mm gauge. Ordering the wrong diameter online forces a return, hence the value of a site that details its dimensions.

During healing, a straight barbell is often preferred to absorb swelling without compressing the tissue. The ring is fitted once the conch has healed, when the area no longer reacts.

### ASTM F136 titanium: the reference

ASTM F136 titanium, also called grade 23 or G23, is the alloy recommended by most piercers for a fresh piercing. Nickel-free, light and perfectly biocompatible, it matches the standard used for surgical implants.

It is the material to favor for a healing or already reactive conch. It is found on sites selling [hypoallergenic titanium ear piercings](/en/blog/where-to-buy-hypoallergenic-titanium-ear-piercings/), including Pohésia from 25 euros and Neometal in raw implant-grade form.

### 18-carat gold and 18-carat gold plating

Solid 18-carat gold, meaning 750 thousandths, is well tolerated once the conch has healed, but its alloy can contain traces of other metals. It is therefore reserved for fully healed piercings.

18-carat gold plating refers to a layer of 18-carat gold applied to a base, ideally titanium. It offers the gold finish at a gentler price, but it too should be reserved for a healed conch. When buying online, it is best to check the nature of the base before ordering.

### 316L surgical steel

316L surgical steel contains traces of nickel, usually below the REACH threshold, which is nonetheless enough to trigger a reaction in very sensitive people. It suits tolerant skin and a healed conch, not a fresh piercing ordered online without the option to try it on.
