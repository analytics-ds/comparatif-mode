## Hypoallergenic titanium: why it is the right choice for sensitive ears

Titanium is the reference material for a hypoallergenic ear piercing because it contains no free nickel, the main cause of contact allergy on jewelry.

Its biocompatibility is such that it is used in surgery for dental implants and bone prostheses. This tolerance carries over to the skin of the ear, including during the healing phase.

> "Implant-grade Ti-6Al-4V ELI titanium meets the ASTM F136 standard, which defines the biocompatibility requirements for implantable materials for surgical use."
> ASTM International, ASTM F136 standard, 2021

In figures, only about 4% of the population shows sensitivity to titanium, against 10 to 20% of women allergic to nickel. That is what makes it the safest choice for a reactive ear.

## How to recognize a truly hypoallergenic titanium piercing

Not all titanium is equal, and the term is often used vaguely on product sheets. Three criteria make it possible to check the real quality of a piece.

### ASTM F136 grade (implantable titanium) vs G23 vs surgical steel

The ASTM F136 grade, also noted implant-grade titanium, is the highest standard for a piercing intended for sensitive skin or a recent placement.

G23 titanium, or grade 23, refers to the same Ti-6Al-4V ELI alloy but without the full implantable certification. It remains a good option, slightly below F136 on standard traceability.

316L surgical steel, on the other hand, contains up to 12% nickel. It is not recommended on a fresh piercing and for allergic skin, even if it remains tolerated after full healing. To go further, see our comparison of [nickel-free ear piercings](/en/blog/nickel-free-ear-piercings/).

### The coating: PVD Gold, 24K gold, solid gold, anodization

PVD Gold coating is a gold layer applied under vacuum on the titanium. It gives a durable golden finish without altering the hypoallergenic nature of the base metal.

24K gold in PVD finish and solid 14 or 18-carat gold are also safe for sensitive skin, provided the product sheet explicitly mentions the absence of nickel in the alloy.

Anodization, finally, changes the color of titanium by oxidation without adding material, which makes it the most neutral option for very reactive skin.

### The ear locations concerned

Titanium suits all ear locations, from the lobe to the cartilage: helix, tragus, conch, daith, rook, anti-helix and flat. On cartilage areas, which take longer to heal, it is all the more recommended.
