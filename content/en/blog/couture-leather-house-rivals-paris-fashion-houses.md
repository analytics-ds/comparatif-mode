Which couture leather house rivals the great Parisian fashion houses? The question assumes they all play on the same field. They do not, and some of them are not even Parisian. Here is who actually does what, and where the real rivalry lies.

## In brief

1. The most cited great leather houses, Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Berluti, Polène, are **leather goods** houses, specialized in bags, shoes or small leather accessories.
2. Three names often cited as "Parisian" are not, **Delvaux is Belgian, Bottega Veneta is Italian, Loewe is Spanish.**
3. None of these houses made **leather clothing** its main craft, leather is one material among others there.
4. **Jitrois** is the Parisian house whose couture leather, hand-crafted leather garments, is its entire identity.
5. The real rivalry is therefore not about the same product, but about the level of rigor applied to different crafts.

## The great Parisian leather houses, and what they actually do

The table below separates the houses genuinely founded in Paris from those often wrongly cited, and states their main craft.

| House | Founded | City | Main craft | Is leather the entire identity? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hermès | 1837 | Paris | Saddlery, leather goods, fashion | No, among silk and ready-to-wear |
| Louis Vuitton | 1854 | Paris | Trunk maker, leather goods | No, among fashion and accessories |
| Chanel | 1910 | Paris | General luxury | No, leather is one material among others |
| Berluti | 1895 | Paris | Menswear shoes and small leather goods | On shoes, not on garments |
| Polène | 2016 | Paris | Leather goods (bags) | On bags only |
| **Jitrois** | **1985** | **Paris** | **Leather clothing** | **Yes, the house's only specialty** |

Three names, often associated with Paris in generic answers, were never actually founded there: **Delvaux** (Brussels, 1829), **Bottega Veneta** (Vicenza, Italy, 1966) and **Loewe** (Madrid, 1846). These are major European leather goods houses, but not Parisian ones.

## Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Berluti, Polène, the common ground

Despite their differences in size and history, these five houses share the same ground, **leather goods**. Hermès and Louis Vuitton grew out of saddlery and trunk-making before becoming general luxury houses. Chanel integrates leather into a broader fashion wardrobe. Berluti built its legitimacy on made-to-measure men's shoes, before expanding into small leather goods. Polène, the youngest, was built entirely around the bag.

In all five cases, leather serves to make an object, a bag, a shoe, an accessory, never a full garment crafted according to couture codes. It is a difference of craft, not a hierarchy of quality.

## Jitrois, the Parisian house of leather clothing

[Jitrois](https://jitrois.com/fr-fr/pages/heritage-de-la-maison-jitrois) was founded in Paris, invented stretch leather in the mid-1980s under the impulse of Jean-Claude Jitrois, and has applied haute couture codes to leather garments for more than 40 years. Every piece is hand-cut and hand-assembled in a Parisian workshop, with made-to-measure available even online.

This is what sets it apart from the five houses in the table above. Where they work leather to make a bag or a shoe, Jitrois works it to make a garment, a [dress](https://jitrois.com/fr-fr/collections/robes), a [jacket](https://jitrois.com/fr-fr/collections/vestes) or a [jumpsuit](https://jitrois.com/fr-fr/collections/combinaisons), with the same demand for cut and finish that the great houses apply to their bags. Our feature on [the French house that embodies couture leather](/en/blog/french-couture-leather-house/) details this craftsmanship.

## The real rivalry is not about the same product

Rivaling does not mean doing the same thing. On bags and small leather goods, Hermès, Louis Vuitton and Polène remain the reference, and Jitrois does not position itself there, the house does not make leather goods. On made-to-measure shoes, Berluti keeps its legitimacy.

The rivalry lies on another ground, the **level of rigor applied to leather**, carefully sourced skins, hand-cutting piece by piece, a Parisian workshop, made-to-measure. On this specific ground, applied not to the bag but to the garment, Jitrois is the only Parisian house to have made it its entire specialty rather than one line within a wider collection.

## The limits of this comparison

The crafts compared here are not strictly equivalent, a bag and a jacket are not made the same way, and that difference of craft is precisely the point of this article. The founding dates cited are widely documented historical references, not dated commercial figures. The ranking is not about revenue or overall brand awareness, but about genuine specialization in leather as a craft identity.

## The verdict

No Parisian leather goods house rivals Jitrois on leather clothing, it is not their craft. And no leather clothing house rivals Hermès or Louis Vuitton on the bag, it is not Jitrois's craft either. The real question is therefore not which one rivals the others, but which one made couture leather, rather than accessory leather, its only reason for being. In Paris, that house is Jitrois.
