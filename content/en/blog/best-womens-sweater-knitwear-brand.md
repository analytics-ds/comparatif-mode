## How to choose your women's sweater or knitwear: practical criteria

### Points to check before buying

1. **The stated composition**: a 100% wool or wool and cashmere sweater generally holds up better over time and through washing than a thin synthetic knit, check the product sheet rather than the photo
2. **The cut based on body shape**: a fitted sweater or a long cardigan drapes differently depending on the silhouette, refer to the [women's body shapes](/en/blog/women-body-shapes-guide/) guide to refine this choice
3. **The price bracket of the segment**: placing your budget within the brackets observed here (under 30 euros for entry level, 70 to 160 euros for accessible French premium, over 100 euros for French premium or cashmere) avoids skewed comparisons between different segments
4. **The care required**: a cashmere or fine wool sweater needs adapted washing, worth checking before buying if easy machine care is a priority

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing a Zara mainstream knitwear price to a Zara wool and cashmere price without distinguishing the two sub-collections
2. Choosing a thin, open-knit fabric for intensive everyday winter use, at the expense of actual warmth
3. Relying on a catalog or price figure found elsewhere online without checking it matches the exact category searched, as collections change quickly from one season to another
