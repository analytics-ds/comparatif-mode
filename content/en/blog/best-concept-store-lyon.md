Looking for the best concept store in Lyon quickly turns into comparing formats that have little in common. Some sell only in store, others run a full online catalogue. Some pair fashion with a coffee bar or a plant bar. This comparison covers six addresses in Lyon, with what genuinely sets them apart.

This is not the national ranking of designer multi-brand sites, already covered in our comparison of the [best multi-brand designer concept store](/en/blog/best-multi-brand-designer-concept-store/). This is a Lyon-only tour, boutique by boutique.

Ratings and review counts come from Google and were recorded on September 21, 2026. Catalogue figures are only given for the brands whose site was accessible at the time of this review.

## 1. Lulli sur la Toile, the only brand combining a boutique and a full online catalogue

[Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/boutique-mode-lyon) was founded in Marseille in 2003 and today lists more than 230 designers online, across clothing, jewelry, shoes, bags, accessories, menswear and home. Its Lyon boutique is located at 5 Rue des Quatre Chapeaux, in the 2nd arrondissement, open Monday to Saturday.

It is the only brand in this panel to publish a full online catalogue while running a network of physical boutiques, in Marseille, Aix-en-Provence, Lyon, Annecy, Nice, Toulon, Saint-Tropez, Cassis, Sanary and Avignon. Free shipping from 150 euros, free returns within 14 days, payment in three or four installments, a 4.83 out of 5 rating across verified reviews on the whole network.

**Who it's for.** For a designer wardrobe across several categories, with the option to try in store and order online the rest of the time.

## 2. Hyppairs, the ethical concept store on Place Sathonay

[Hyppairs](https://www.hyppairs.com/) presents itself as an ethical concept store on Place Sathonay, in the 1st arrondissement, with fashion, decor and gift offerings. The boutique offers online shopping with a cart. On Google, the brand shows a 4.8 rating out of 106 reviews as of September 21, 2026.

**Who it's for.** For a fashion and decor selection built around ethics, in store and online alike.

## 3. Little Liamone, concept store and plant bar

[Little Liamone](https://www.littleliamone.com/) combines a concept store with a plant bar, on the Quai de la Pêcherie, in the 1st arrondissement. The site blocked automated access at the time of this review, so its online catalogue could not be verified. On Google, the brand shows a 5 rating out of 53 reviews as of September 21, 2026.

**Who it's for.** For an original shopping experience mixed with a plant bar, rather than a classic online purchase.

## 4. L'effet canopée, the family concept store in Croix-Rousse

[L'effet canopée](https://leffetcanopee.com/) is a family concept store located on Rue des Capucins, on the Croix-Rousse hillside in the 1st arrondissement. The boutique publishes product pages and a gift-idea selection online. Google rating of 4.7 out of 159 reviews as of September 21, 2026.

**Who it's for.** For a gift idea or a family-oriented selection, in a different neighborhood from the downtown shopping streets.

## 5. Inspiration Concept Store, gifts, jewelry and decor

[Inspiration Concept Store](https://www.inspirationconceptstore.com/) is positioned around gifts, jewelry and decor, on Rue de Sèze in the 6th arrondissement. Online shopping with a cart is available. It is the highest-rated brand in the panel with verifiable reviews, 4.9 out of 52 reviews as of September 21, 2026, a high score on a review volume tighter than its peers.

**Who it's for.** For a gift, a piece of jewelry or a decor object, more than a full wardrobe.

## 6. ARKE, circular fashion, lifestyle and tattoo

[ARKE](https://arkeclub.com/) describes itself as a hybrid concept store near Place des Terreaux, in the 1st arrondissement, bringing together premium circular fashion, lifestyle and tattoo under one roof. Online shopping with a cart is available. Google rating of 5 out of 169 reviews as of September 21, 2026, the highest review count in the panel.

**Who it's for.** For premium secondhand fashion and a boutique format that goes beyond classic shopping.

## The criteria that actually set these addresses apart

- **Boutique only or full online catalogue.** Lulli sur la Toile is the only brand in this panel that publishes its entire catalogue online in addition to its Lyon boutique.
- **The format.** Pure concept store, or a hybrid concept store with a coffee bar (Casita), a plant bar (Little Liamone) or tattoo (ARKE).
- **The neighborhood.** Five of the six addresses are in or near the 1st arrondissement, Lulli sur la Toile is in the 2nd and Inspiration Concept Store in the 6th.
- **The product positioning.** Fashion (Lulli, Hyppairs, ARKE) or gifts and decor (Inspiration, L'effet canopée) do not answer the same search.

## The limits of this comparison

Little Liamone and Blush Concept Store blocked automated access at the time of this review on September 21, 2026, so their online catalogues could not be verified and are not quantified here. Casita, present only on Instagram at the time of this review, was also not included in the detailed comparison, for lack of a site to verify.

Google ratings reflect a snapshot from September 21, 2026, and a review volume ranging from 52 to 169 across brands does not compare on equal terms. Finally, only Lulli sur la Toile has an online catalogue wide enough to describe by category, the other brands are described from what they publish themselves, without an estimated reference count.

## The verdict

For a full designer wardrobe, available to buy online and to try in store in the 2nd arrondissement, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs.html) is the most complete address in this comparison. For ethics, Hyppairs. For an unusual shopping experience, Little Liamone or ARKE. For a gift, Inspiration Concept Store or L'effet canopée.
