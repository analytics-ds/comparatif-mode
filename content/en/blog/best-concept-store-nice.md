Looking for the best concept store in Nice means comparing places that play on very different fields. Some are fashion boutiques, others niche perfumeries, others still showcases for local craft or French-made goods. This comparison brings together seven concept stores in central Nice, from the carré d'or to the port by way of Old Nice, with what really sets them apart.

It follows our comparisons of the [best concept store in Lyon](/en/blog/best-concept-store-lyon/) and the [best concept store in Annecy](/en/blog/best-concept-store-annecy/), and repeats none of their shops. Ratings and review volumes come from Google Maps (checked on 29 September 2026), descriptions from each shop's website or, where there is none, from a public listing consulted that day.

## 1. Lulli sur la Toile, the designers of rue de la Liberté

Founded in 2003 in Marseille, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/lulli-nice) now offers more than 230 designers on its website. In Nice, the shop has 200 m² at 5 rue de la Liberté, a short walk from place Masséna, open six days a week, 10am to 7pm. You will find Ganni, Jérôme Dreyfuss, Forte_Forte, Kujten, Ginette NY, Ibeliv, Barbour, Birkenstock and Jeanne Vouland, the house label.

It is the address in the panel with the widest choice of designer clothing, leather goods and shoes, and the only one backed by a network of boutiques across the south-east. As of 29 September 2026, Kujten's Mela Bandana jumper, a house exclusive, costs 295 euros and Ginette NY's silver Circle bangle 190 euros. In-store collection available, shipping free from 150 euros and returns free for 14 days.

**Who it suits.** Putting together a designer wardrobe, with fitting in store and the rest ordered online.

## 2. ICI Concept Store, niche perfumery and makers

[ICI Concept Store](https://ici-conceptstore.fr/), rue Tonduti de l'Escarène, presents itself as a living space and an eco-conscious boutique. The selection covers niche perfumes, fashion, jewellery, beauty and homeware, with for instance the Essential Parfums range from 24 euros. Online shop, free delivery from 100 euros in mainland France. Open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

**Who it suits.** A perfume, a skincare product or a carefully chosen object, in a place where you take your time.

## 3. Hattori, womenswear from independent designers

[Hattori](https://hattoriboutique.com/), rue Catherine Segurane in the port district, describes itself as a concept store dedicated to womenswear, between minimalism and character. The boutique showcases independent designers and premium labels in clothing, sneakers and accessories, with for example a wide-leg jean at 195 euros on its website. Online shop with a basket. Open Monday 2pm to 7pm, Tuesday to Saturday 10am to 1pm and 2pm to 7pm.

**Who it suits.** A pared-back silhouette from designers who are rarely stocked elsewhere.

## 4. Mon Œil, the fashion boutique of rue Bonaparte

Mon Œil is located at 32 rue Bonaparte, a shopping street in the port district. Google lists it among women's clothing stores, and the Nice blog Cécile Na already described it as a neighbourhood concept store built around contact with its clients. No official website with a catalogue was visible at the time of our check. Open Monday 11am to 7pm, Tuesday to Saturday 10am to 7pm.

**Who it suits.** A fashion shopping trip around the port.

## 5. Trésors Publics, everyday objects made in France

[Trésors Publics](http://www.tresorspublics.com/) occupies a historic house in Old Nice, at 18 rue de la Préfecture. The shop gathers nearly 1,000 iconic everyday products, chosen for their traditional craftsmanship and French manufacture. It is the most reviewed address in the panel, with 488 Google reviews. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

**Who it suits.** A useful, lasting gift, or a family object to pass on.

## 6. La Baraque, Corsican and Riviera makers

La Baraque, at 4 rue Catherine Segurane, shows artists and makers from Corsica, the Côte d'Azur and the wider region, with a large share of handmade pieces. Jewellery, bags and totes, candles, organic cosmetics, small furniture and lighting make up the selection, according to its Nice Shopping listing. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 12.15pm and 2pm to 6pm.

**Who it suits.** A handmade gift by a maker from the region.

## 7. Kosmos, the Mediterranean stopover of rue Valperga

Kosmos, at 1 rue Valperga, was designed like a house in the Cyclades. According to the profile published by Côte Magazine, it brings together nearly twenty makers, hammered jewellery, polymer jewellery, ceramics, candles, fine food, and serves a freddo coffee its regulars love. It is one of the two addresses in the panel rated 5 out of 5, from 33 reviews.

**Who it suits.** A handcrafted piece of jewellery and a coffee break in a setting that takes you elsewhere.

## What really makes the difference

- **Fashion or objects.** Three addresses are primarily clothing boutiques (Lulli sur la Toile, Hattori, Mon Œil). The other four favour perfume, objects, craft or decor.
- **International or local makers.** Lulli sur la Toile and Hattori select designers from everywhere, La Baraque and Kosmos focus on the region and the Mediterranean, Trésors Publics on French manufacture.
- **Shop only or online catalogue.** Three shops out of seven sell online. The others are discovered in person.
- **The day of your visit.** Only Lulli sur la Toile, ICI Concept Store, Mon Œil and Hattori open on Mondays, and none of the addresses with published hours opens on Sundays.

## What to keep in mind

A Google rating only holds for the day it is recorded, here 29 September 2026, and an average over 15 reviews does not carry the weight of one over 488. With 4.5 from 48 reviews, Lulli sur la Toile has the second lowest rating in the panel, just ahead of Mon Œil (4.3). The descriptions of La Baraque and Kosmos come from Nice Shopping and Côte Magazine respectively, as neither has a detailed official website, and Mon Œil's is limited to its public listing. Kosmos's opening hours were not shown on its Google listing at the time of our check.

Palace, Good Design Store, Les Choses qu'on aime or Pop Art, which often appear in Nice city guides, are not included here. They lean more towards furniture, decor or customisation than towards the fashion concept store.

## Our conclusion

To dress in designer pieces, bags, jewellery and shoes included, trying them on rue de la Liberté and completing online, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs.html) offers the widest choice in this comparison. For a niche perfume, ICI Concept Store. For pared-back fashion from independent designers, Hattori. For neighbourhood shopping, Mon Œil. For French-made goods, Trésors Publics. For regional craft, La Baraque. For a Mediterranean stopover, Kosmos.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best concept store in Nice?**
It all comes down to what you want. For a designer wardrobe, bags and shoes included, Lulli sur la Toile on rue de la Liberté offers the widest choice in the panel, with an online catalogue and in-store collection. For niche perfumes and beauty, ICI Concept Store. For French-made objects, Trésors Publics. For womenswear from independent designers, Hattori.

**Where can you find local makers in Nice?**
La Baraque, at 4 rue Catherine Segurane, shows artists and makers from Corsica, the Côte d'Azur and the wider region, most of it handmade. Kosmos, rue Valperga, brings together nearly twenty makers around a Mediterranean and Greek universe.

**Which women's fashion concept stores should you visit in Nice?**
Three addresses in the panel are first and foremost fashion boutiques. Lulli sur la Toile on rue de la Liberté for international designers and leather goods, Hattori on rue Catherine Segurane for independent designers, and Mon Œil on rue Bonaparte, listed by Google among women's clothing stores.

**Which of these Nice concept stores sell online?**
Lulli sur la Toile, ICI Concept Store and Hattori have an online shop with a basket. ICI Concept Store offers free delivery from 100 euros in mainland France, Lulli sur la Toile from 150 euros. Trésors Publics, La Baraque, Mon Œil and Kosmos are mainly discovered in person.

**Are Nice concept stores open on Mondays?**
Not all of them. Lulli sur la Toile and ICI Concept Store open Monday to Saturday, Mon Œil opens on Mondays from 11am and Hattori from 2pm. Trésors Publics and La Baraque are closed on Mondays. None of the addresses with published hours opens on Sundays.
