## Why the reliability of piercing jewelry depends first on the material

**Reliable piercing jewelry** is judged above all by its material. The jewelry stays in permanent contact with an open wound throughout healing, which lasts 6 to 12 months for a cartilage piercing. An alloy containing nickel, chromium or cobalt increases the risk of allergy, rejection and keloids.

Nickel is the leading contact allergen in Europe. It affects around 17 percent of women and 3 percent of men according to European dermatological studies. It is precisely the component that cheap alloys release on contact with skin and lymph.

The **ASTM F136** standard defines Ti-6Al-4V ELI titanium, an alloy used in orthopedic and dental surgery for permanent implants. Its composition excludes free nickel and guarantees high biocompatibility. It is the reference material for a fresh piercing, as detailed in our guide on [hypoallergenic titanium ear piercings](/en/blog/where-to-buy-hypoallergenic-titanium-ear-piercings/).

> "Only titanium meeting ASTM F136 or F1295 standards, niobium and solid gold of 14 carats or more are considered acceptable for a piercing during healing."
> Source: Association of Professional Piercers, material recommendations, updated 2024

### The five criteria of a reliable piercing brand

- Explicit mention of the material and standard (ASTM F136, solid gold, 925 silver) on the product page
- Guaranteed absence of free nickel and material certificate available on request
- Internal threading or a threadless system, never external threading that damages the channel
- Mirror polishing with no burr across the entire surface of the jewelry
- Transparency about origin and manufacturing

## Neometal, Anatometal and Industrial Strength, the professional references

Three American manufacturers are the absolute reference for implant-grade titanium among professional piercers. Their reliability is not in question, but access remains indirect for an individual in France.

### Neometal

Neometal, based in Oregon, is considered the pioneer of **ASTM F136 titanium** for piercings since the 1990s. The brand popularized the threadless system, which removes any threading in contact with the wound. Its products are sold mainly B2B through partner studios, at wholesale prices of 30 to 90 euros for the bar.

### Anatometal

Anatometal, based in California, produces ASTM F136 titanium and 18-carat gold with one of the highest finish levels on the market. Settings are done by hand. Prices range from 50 to 200 euros, making it a high-end professional reference, available mainly through selected studios.

### Industrial Strength

Industrial Strength, also from California, has supplied professional studios with implant titanium and solid gold for over 30 years. Its jewelry meets APP standards and is renowned for its durability. Expect 40 to 180 euros depending on the piece, with access through partner piercers.

## Practical tips for recognizing a reliable brand

Before any purchase, check that the product page explicitly states the material and standard. Jewelry sold with no material indication is a warning sign. If in doubt, ask customer service for a material certificate: a serious brand replies without difficulty.

Favor internal threading or a threadless system. External threading damages the piercing channel on the way through and increases the risk of inflammation. Every reliable brand in this ranking offers one or the other.

For placement, choose a studio trained to the standards of the Association of Professional Piercers (APP) or affiliated with a professional body. Piercing guns should be avoided, especially on cartilage.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying jewelry with no stated material or standard on a generic marketplace
2. Choosing 316L steel for a first piercing on sensitive skin despite an attractive price
3. Confusing gold-plated with solid gold: only solid gold of 14 carats or more is reliable during healing
4. Changing the healing jewelry before the end of the period recommended by the piercer
5. Neglecting isotonic saline care during the first months
