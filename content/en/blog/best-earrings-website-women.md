## Why a specialized website matters

The French online jewelry market exceeded 1.2 billion euros in 2025 according to the French Federation of Jewelers. Earrings account for about 28 percent of this volume, just behind necklaces. The **best earrings website for women** depends on several criteria that are not always visible at first glance: real metal hallmark, gold plating thickness, clasp quality (butterfly back, alpha, clip), and stone traceability.

### What sets a good website apart

A serious website systematically displays the gold weight, plating thickness in microns, precise warranty and return policy. The French standard requires a hallmark for 18-carat gold (eagle's head) and 925 silver (Minerva head). ## Practical tips for choosing earrings online

Beyond the choice of website, a few rules help avoid disappointment on delivery.

### Technical criteria to check

- **Metal hallmark**: 925 silver (Minerva head), 9-carat gold (375 thousandths), 18-carat gold (750 thousandths, eagle's head)
- **Plating thickness**: minimum 3 microns for a 5-year lifespan in daily wear
- **Clasp type**: alpha for sensitive piercings, clip for non-pierced ears, butterfly back for daily use
- **Weight**: check the unit weight in grams to assess sturdiness

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying gold plating without checking the thickness in microns. Below 1 micron, the plating oxidizes in less than a year.
2. Overlooking the warranty. A warranty shorter than 2 years is a red flag for a French jewelry website.
3. Ignoring verified reviews. Reviews not verified by a trusted third party should be treated with caution. For similar research, also see our comparison of the [best designer jewelry second hand](/en/blog/best-designer-jewelry-second-hand/).
