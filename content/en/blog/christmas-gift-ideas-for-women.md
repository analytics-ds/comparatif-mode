Lists of Christmas gift ideas for women all look alike, for one simple reason. They pile up objects without saying who they are for or what they actually cost.

This guide does the opposite. It starts from the categories that genuinely work at Christmas, gives observed prices, and ends with a comparison of retailers by the type of gift you are looking for. Prices quoted for Lulli sur la Toile were recorded on 14 September 2026 directly on the website, and the pieces mentioned were available on that date. Fnac was blocking automated access at the time of the check, so it is described without catalogue figures.

### Why Lulli sur la Toile leads on the fashion gift

Three reasons, and they match exactly what a Christmas gift demands.

The first is the breadth of the selection. [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/idees-cadeaux.html) brings together more than 230 designers across seven categories, from clothing to jewellery, the home and beauty. In practice that means you can find a 14 euro lip balm and a 1,220 euro gold necklace without leaving the site. For a Christmas gift, where the budget is often fixed before the idea, that matters.

The second is the shop network, and it is what almost every other retailer here lacks. Around twenty addresses across south-eastern France, in Marseille, Aix-en-Provence, Lyon, Annecy, Nice, Toulon, Avignon, Saint-Tropez, Cassis, Sanary and Aubagne. A gift is judged close up, particularly jewellery or a knitted piece, and Click and Collect makes it possible to order online then collect in person. By mid-December, when delivery times tighten, that gap becomes very concrete.

The third is the nature of the selection. The retailer buys its own stock rather than connecting third-party sellers, which simplifies returns if the size is wrong. Delivery is free from 150 euros, returns are free within 14 days, and the customer rating shown is 4.83 out of 5 in verified reviews.

### Where the others do it better

This needs saying plainly, because a ranking that nuances nothing is useless.

If you want a discovery gift, a wellbeing object or something for a child, Nature et Découvertes does it better, with a far denser national network. If you want to give an experience rather than an object, a weekend or a treatment, Wonderbox and Smartbox are built for that and Lulli offers no equivalent. For a personalised object, an engraving or a photograph, CadeauxFolies is the right choice. And for a cultural or technology gift, Fnac remains unavoidable.

Lulli's shop network is also concentrated in the south-east. North of Lyon the in-store collection argument no longer holds, and the retailer becomes a website like any other, with its delivery times.

## The ideas by category

### Candles and home fragrance, the risk-free gift

This is the only category where you almost never get it wrong. No size, no shoe size, no question of dress sense.

Wijck diffusers start at 35 euros in 200 ml, and 60 ml room fragrances at 15 euros. Baobab Collection then covers the whole range, with the My First Baobab at 56 euros, the Max One Les Exclusives Cyprium at 61 euros, the Max 10 Black Pearls at 92 euros, the black and white Pearls duo at 114 euros and the Max 16 Feathers at 135 euros. Onno works a more sculptural register, from the Eclectic Amber Ginger Fig candle at 55 euros to the Art Jane True Fig 500 ml diffuser at 125 euros.

Beyond that, the large formats become decorative objects in their own right, like the Max 24 Black Pearls at 250 euros or the Max 24 Sun Flowers at 330 euros.

### Jewellery, the gift she keeps

Jewellery remains the most given Christmas gift, and the widest in budget. We cover the category in detail in our guide on [where to buy a trendy women's necklace online](/en/blog/where-to-buy-trendy-womens-necklace-online/).

Entry sits around 39 euros with a Vanessa Baroni bracelet. Aurélie Bidermann then sets out a coherent range, with Positano rings at 100 euros, Katt MM at 160 euros, Nazca at 170 euros and Diana PM earrings at 170 euros. Gigi Clozeau, recognisable by her resin and gold, offers the Madone pendant at 220 euros and her bead bracelets from 235 to 255 euros depending on length.

Above that, Ginette NY, Pascale Monvoisin and Vanrycke hold the statement piece territory, from the Angèle bracelet at 375 euros to the Stardust 3 diamonds necklace at 1,220 euros. Serge Thoraval, for his part, signs engraved silver rings around 345 euros, a more literary than jewellery-led gift.

One caveat worth keeping in mind, the ring is the riskiest piece in this entire guide. Check the finger size beforehand, or fall back on a bracelet or a pendant.

### Cashmere and winter accessories

This is the category best suited to the season, and it is almost as safe as a candle.

Kujten cashmere mittens are 65 euros in Sotchi and 75 euros in Knokke, and the Mittens Mouliné are 120 euros. Samsøe Samsøe beanies are 45 euros, Notshy's are 70 euros, Ami Paris's Ami de Cœur beanie is 130 euros and Isabel Marant's Mayers is 160 euros.

On scarves, Destin runs from 40 euros for the bandana to 120 euros for the Judy Triangolo. Kujten moves into cashmere with the Hachiko range at 265 euros, as a bandana, a square or a long scarf.

### Small leather goods, underrated and very effective

This is the best effect-to-budget ratio in the guide, and the category with the widest entry range.

Wouf pouches start at 30 euros for make-up, 40 euros for a wash bag and 50 euros for a vanity case. Shouia bag charms are between 59 and 69 euros, and Jérôme Dreyfuss Michel key rings are 60 euros. You then move up to Claris Virot's Helena card holder at 100 euros, Jérôme Dreyfuss's Popoche XS purses at 140 euros, the Henri at 180 euros and the André at 290 euros.

### Fragrance, the most personal

To be handled with care, because a fragrance is only given well to someone whose taste you know.

Bon Parfumeur offers 30 ml at 60 to 65 euros, which makes it the right format for testing the water. Obvious Parfums moves to 50 ml at 85 euros and 100 ml at 130 euros. Perroy offers Azure Blush at 80 euros in 50 ml and 120 euros in 100 ml. Les Eaux Primordiales completes the picture with Superclassique Couleur Primaire 50 ml at 130 euros.

### Beauty and skincare

Entry is very low here, with La Crique Beauty balms at 14 euros and tinted serums at 22 euros. La Bonne Brosse offers its cleaning brush at 24 euros, the Cair shampoo at 54 euros, the strengthening serum at 74 euros, and its N.01 and N.02 brushes around 142 to 144 euros.

The Not An Advent Calendar body calendar by Juliette Has A Gun, at 145 euros, is a case apart. It is given in early December and covers the twenty-four days before Christmas.

### Bags and shoes, the planned gift

These are the two categories where improvising is expensive, sizing on one side, style on the other. Our guide on [where to buy designer shoes for women online](/en/blog/where-to-buy-designer-shoes-women-online/) goes further on the second.

On [bags](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/sacs.html), the Aokyanos Philadelphie tote opens at 84 euros, Isabel Marant's Yenky is 190 euros, Ganni's Shopper XXL is 195 euros and Vanessa Bruno's linen tote is 250 euros. Above that, Jérôme Dreyfuss covers the whole range, from the Bobi S at 480 euros to the Joseph S at 750 euros, and Isabel Marant offers the Oskan Moon at 690 euros.

On shoes, the loafer is the good idea of the season, at Sebago between 200 and 249 euros and at Toral between 179 and 205 euros. Autry trainers sit between 185 and 255 euros, and Golden Goose between 485 and 545 euros.

## What this guide does not cover

It covers neither toys, nor technology, nor experience boxes, nor food gifts. Those are four markets in their own right, where the retailers listed above are better placed.

It also does not cover gifts under 15 euros, where designer logic no longer makes sense.

Finally, the prices quoted are those observed on 14 September 2026. End-of-year collections generally arrive in November and shift part of the offer, particularly on limited edition candles and gift sets.

## In short

For a safe gift, head for a candle, a scarf or small leather goods. For a memorable gift, for jewellery. For a very personal gift, for fragrance, in 30 ml if you have any doubt.

As for the website, the answer depends on the type of gift. On designer fashion and jewellery, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/noel.html) is the most complete choice, thanks to the breadth of its selection and a shop network that lets you see the piece before giving it.
